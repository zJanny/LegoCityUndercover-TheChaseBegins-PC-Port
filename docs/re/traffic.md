The traffic manager 0x527410 spawns and removes the level's `Traffic%02u` vehicles. The AI driver input 0x26d934 steers the spawned cars. The pedestrian manager 0x51b33c is in [pedestrians.md](pedestrians.md); the manager swap is corrected in [pedestrians.md §2026-10-20T11:17:42](pedestrians.md#ts-20261020-111742). At the flip the node position equals driver +0x48 .. +0x50. Driver speeds +0x68 of the ten cars are 8.6 to 11.3. The spawns used two routes (driver +4 = 0x8dec78c with signal object 0x8b33bf0, and 0x8dec764 with 0x8b37db0). The player stood at (900.5491, 16.5672, 1110.28) and the camera eye at (910.5491, 20.4672, 1110.2267) at all flips.

**Port.** `TrafficSystem` loads the cars (`Traffic01..32`), the limit, the shuffled order and the fade bounds, parses the level's `Pregen/Traffic` file at the scene enter, derives direction, room and floor cell, and runs the 64 prefill updates; the update runs in the system phase after the pedestrian manager. A spawn places the car's vehicle at the point (`placeVehicleAt`), sets its record state to 1 (no kinematic switch, no physics body is made) and enables its entity; a removal disables it. EnableTrafficAndPedestrians and SetTrafficMaxVehicles reach the manager before its update. In a run of `chase-game` from the start of lvl_01 six cars appear in the prefill and about one car appears or leaves every 25 updates after that (the port's own numbers). The stand-ins are TRAFFIC-S1, TRAFFIC-S7 and TRAFFIC-S8 in [pedestrians-traffic-cars.md](../plans/features/pedestrians-traffic-cars.md).
