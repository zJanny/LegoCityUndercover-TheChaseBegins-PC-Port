# Gameplay camera: the vehicle camera

The camera while the player drives: preset chooser 0x1597c8, the InVehicle preset block, the record's `ATTR_Camera*` floats, the input provider, the automatic turn and the pitch clamp. Entries are dated; new entries go at the end.

**Chooser 0x1597c8 (called every update from 0x2e5158).** Reads the player controller (`*0x3772b8 + 0x7c`), asks `0x24255c` for the vehicle (ctrl+0x6c is 0x11c or 0x19b, else none) and sets the camera system object's target and preset through 0x23f488(K, target, preset): K[0] = target entity, K+0x160 = preset pointer, K+0x158 = *0x3770bc. Nothing is reset: the follow controller keeps its wanted and tracked points, so entering and leaving only change the target and the preset (a change of target also sets K+0x150 = 1.0 and calls 0x229e54, not read).
- On foot: the OnFoot block 0x376f6c, or the Skydive / other blocks for other player states (0x2a, 0x2b, 5, 0xd7..0xda, 0x80..0x8c; not read further).
- In a vehicle: the 0x70-byte InVehicle block at 0x376fdc is copied to 0x514170 and that copy is the preset. When the vehicle entity's type byte (+0x12) is 183 or 243 and the record's byte +0x311 has bit 0 (`ATTR_CameraOverride`), the record floats go into the middle values of the triples: +0x2f0 PosY into the height triple (block +0x10), +0x2f4 PosZ into the distance triple (+0x04), +0x2f8 LookY into the look lift triple (+0x20), +0x2fc LookZ into the look depth triple (+0x2c). For record types (+0x2ec) 0x2b, 0x2d, 0x2e and 0x2f the automatic turn gain (block +0x38) is set to 0. `ATTR_CameraPanScale` (+0x300) is not read here.

**InVehicle block 0x376fdc** (offsets from the block start): distance (4.5, 5.5, 6.5), height (0.2, 0.35, 0.5), ground offset 0.25, look lift (-0.5, -0.5, -0.5), look depth (1.5, 1.5, 1.5), +0x34 = 0 (hold time of the automatic turn after a shoulder press), **+0x38 = 1.0 automatic turn gain**, +0x3c = 1.5, yaw gain 0.6, +0x44 = 3.5, **position smoothing base 0.7, look smoothing base 0** (the look point follows at once), 2.0, 1.0, 5.0, 2.5, 1.25 (the sweep constants of OnFoot), pitch limits 0.25 and 0.2, bytes +0x6c..: +0x6e = 1 (vehicle layout; OnFoot has +0x6c = 1). The police car record: PosY -0.2, PosZ 5.0, LookY 0.4, LookZ 0.5 (oracle copy at 0x514170: distance (4.5, 5.0, 6.5), height (0.2, -0.2, 0.5), look lift (-0.5, 0.4, -0.5), look depth (1.5, 0.5, 1.5)).

**Input provider 0x32f334 and the pre-step 0x330e4c with the vehicle layout (preset byte +0x6e set).** `in+0x40` (body height) is the target entity's `+0x64 + +0x70`, the centre y plus the half height of its collision box: the root node of the vehicle model's first collision item (police trooper: 0.0801 + 0.826 = 0.9061). The provider sets `in+0xfc` = 10.0 and `in+0x100` = 2.5, `in+0x48` = 1 and clears `in+0x44..46`. 0x330e4c then adds 10 to the distance and 2.5 to the height (D = 5.0 + 10 = 15, H = -0.2 + 2.5 = 2.3 for the police car), and the look lift is `lookHeight + 2 x [node +0xb0] - bodyUp` instead of `lookHeight + body - bodyUp`, where node +0xb0 is the half height of the model's mesh bounds (police trooper 0.99219 = the union of its three meshes, `ModelMesh::half`; node +0xa0 .. +0xb4 hold centre (0, 0.0778, -0.08) and half extents (1.0494, 0.992, 2.827)) and bodyUp = body - ground offset (0.656). Everything is multiplied by the clearance S+4 as on foot. Oracle at rest behind the car: wanted position 15.0 m behind (1.39, 4.79, 14.94 from the car origin), look point = car + (0, 0.656, 0) plus the look offset (0.5 m ahead along the car's forward axis, y 1.728 = 0.4 + 2 x 0.992 - 0.656). The target matrix is the vehicle node's world matrix (node +0x5c, rows of 16 bytes, translation +0x8c).

**Automatic turn (0x3310d0, new for this preset).** With the hold timer S+0xc and the shoulder accumulator K+0x140 both 0: v = A - target position, expressed in the target frame (0x20c7f0: x = v . row 0, z = v . row 2); a = atan2(x, -z) (0 when the camera is straight behind); the wanted turn is 0.25 |a| a, limited to +-dt x gain, multiplied by max(0.4 - S+0x1c, 0) x 2. It is added to the shoulder and stick terms before the rotation of A about B. S+0x1c is the side push timer (set to 0.5 by a side push, counted down by dt only while the look point moves, 0x330f8c).

**Stick and shoulders.** The stick term of 0x200ae0 is cleared when the vehicle has type byte 183 (0x200b0c): a full stick does not turn the camera while driving (oracle, circle pad x 120 for 60 updates). The shoulder terms work as on foot.

**Pitch clamp 0x2e0a50, now transcribed (CAMERA-14).** Applies when `in+0x48` is 0 and `in+0x60` (= S+4 x S+8 at the pre-step) is above 0.99, so on foot and not in vehicles. The look height L = B tracker y + C tracker y is clamped between `head - lowGain x depth` and `feet + highGain x depth`, where feet = the target's y, head = y + body height, depth = max(10, (head - eye tracker) . unit(look - eye tracker)) with the look point (B tracker x, L, B tracker z), the gains are block +0x64 (`in+0xd8`, 0.25) for the head side and +0x68 (`in+0xdc`) for the feet side; the B tracker y becomes the result minus the C tracker y. Oracle: jumping into the police car (the player drops 2.3 m in 20 updates) and out of it moved the look point up to 0.45 away from the unclamped law; with the clamp the replay is within 1.4e-3.

**Enter and leave (oracle `m11-2-patrol-car` with the measuring aids above, dt 0.033).** The preset switch happens in the update in which ctrl+0x6c becomes 0x11c (Drive; the jump in, 0x11a, still follows the player): K[0] changes to the vehicle and K+0x160 to 0x514170 in that update, the camera continues from its tracked state. Leaving: the update in which the state leaves 0x11c (0x11b) follows the player again with the OnFoot block. While the release script of mission 1-1 pushes its director camera (about 4 updates after Drive begins) the follow command is not on the stack and K is frozen. `camera-vehicle-real-data` replays 35 updates of the jump in (eye error 0, look 1.9e-6) and 92 updates of the jump out (eye 0, look 1.4e-4), and the drive (idle, A held, circle pad x 120, coast; 190 updates): eye and look error at most 4.8e-3 after 12 settling updates (the sweep state of the camera object is not restored), 6e-5 to 2e-3 from update 60 on.

**Hand-back after the release director camera (oracle `tools/oracle/camera-vehicle-handback.txt`, 380 updates after X).** The director command (routine 0x2665e4) takes the stack about 28 updates after the press (the follow command leaves it and K is frozen with the wanted position 15.1 m away) and ends at update 297: 1b4864 pushes the follow command again.
