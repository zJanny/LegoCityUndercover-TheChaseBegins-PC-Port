## 2026-10-15T08:15:49+02:00 — Getting into and out of a vehicle: states, timing, paths, events (VEH-4)

Sources: `llvm-objdump` on `artifacts/game/game.arm.elf` (REA was not used) and the Azahar oracle (New 3DS, OpenGL, `--rpc` and `--rpc-input` on port 24713, a sampler that patches the pad reader like the tool and reads the player controller, node and vehicle record by RPC about 400 times per second; states `m11-5-in-donut-van`, `m11-2-patrol-car`, `in-vehicle`, `street-after-1-1`; the police car was unlocked and the player teleported next to it by RPC writes, as measuring aids). **Enter.** X pressed (edge) on foot: event 200 → 0x322a04 (rules in [§2026-10-14T22:44:38](vehicles-system.md#ts-20261014-224438)). Holding X from before the press enters once and does not leave again while it is held; leaving needs a new press. The search 0x225d94 has these exact rules (read, then measured):
- Candidates: entities of type 0xb3, 0xb7, 0xf3, owner flag bit 0 clear, record +0x311 bit 1 clear. The distance (0x270498) uses only the **x and z** excess of the player's node position, taken into the vehicle's frame (0x2536c0), over the entity box (+0x60 centre, +0x6c half): the y component of its result is never written. Squared distance below 4.0 (literal 0x225f4c). Oracle: police car, gap 1.995 m entered, 2.01 m did not (the player stood 0.48 m below the box, which does not count). Height rule (not measured): `|local y - centre y| - (half y + player half y 0.85) <= player centre y 1.15 + 0.25`.
- The entity box of a vehicle is the root node of the first collision item of its model: police car (0, 0.0801, -0.0953) / (0.9696, 0.826, 2.7641) read in the oracle equals it to four decimals; FP_Vehicle_03 (worker) (0, 0.2362, -0.077) / (0.9674, 1.2978, 3.0767) too. `modelCollisionBox`.

**Jump In 0x11a (begin 0x31fffc, update 0x3201b0).** Begin: `0x236a10(entity, 3, ...)` plays animation role 3 (role record 3 of the table at `*0x374294`: `jump`, id 3; variants by class and bit 4 of ctrl+0xe0+0x130 are not read); it saves the player position (ctrl+0x1f8) and heading (u16 ctrl+0x2be), sends message 83 to the vehicle (0x244fc8) and for the global player calls 0x1ea27c(vehicle) and 0x1e7448(1). The position does not change in the begin update. Update k >= 1: with `u = elapsed ticks / animation length` (0x213140, 0x23caa4), position = lerp(start, seat, u) + (0, `*0x3742a0` = 2.1 (the player's jump height) * sin(pi u), 0) (0x221e5c, 0x235dc0) and heading = lerp from the start heading (wrapped to within pi of the target) to atan2 of the vehicle's forward axis (0x24765c, 0x2286d4). The seat is the vehicle node transformed by the record's DriverPosition (+0x27c). The state ends when the elapsed ticks reach the length: **2200 ticks = 11 intervals of the 15 fps jump animation = 22 updates at 100 ticks** (oracle: police car, worker, van_dulmen; the clock is real, so a slow update shortens the count). Oracle fit of the police car path over 21 updates: height of the arc 2.1075, seat y 17.51 at node y 17.81 and DriverPosition y -0.3, worst error 0.0095; worker: seat 1.6 ahead of the node (DriverPosition z 1.6), 22 updates.

**Drive 0x11c begin 0x307abc.** The player node gets flag bits 0x60 (hidden) through 0x23f734(node, 0) (end 0x307bcc and the jump out show it again with `(node, 1)`); message 84 to the vehicle; for the global player two calls 0x23cee8 and 0x262188, 0x1ea27c, 0x1e7448. **PlayerVehicleEntered** is fired here: `0x23cee8(*0x375fe4, vehicle, player, 255, 0)`, and a second time with the object `0x23c188(*(0x376c78 + 0x28))` (not identified). The event names are registered at 0x15b888 into the table 0x375fe0: +0 VehicleChaseEnded, +4 PlayerVehicleEntered, +8 PlayerVehicleExiting, +0xc PlayerVehicleBoundEntered. In lvl_01 the trigger `PlayerVehicleEntered` has the owner `1-1_Police_Car` and starts `1-1_Release_Car_Script` (mission 1-1 step 4). Update 0x307cac sets the player node matrix every update to the physics body's matrix (or, when the body's flag 0xcc bit 1 is set, to the vehicle node's), so the hidden player sits at the **body origin = vehicle node + CentreOfMass** (class value, rotated): police (0, -0.75, 0), donut van (0, -0.9, 0.2), worker (0, -1.15, 0.2), van_dulmen (0, -0.9, 0), each equal to the oracle offsets of the seated player (0.22 ahead and 0.897 below for the donut van, 0.2 ahead and 1.15 below for the worker). In the first Drive update the oracle showed the player 0.1 to 0.2 lower (police y 16.91 against 17.074 later): the body settling, not ported. 0x24255c returns the vehicle only in this state (and 0x19b), so it is 0 during the jumps.

**Vehicle record states (oracle, +2 / +4).** Parked police car and worker: (4, 4) through the whole jump in, (4, 5) in the update Drive begins, (5, 5) from the next update. A vehicle the player drove before (traffic van_dulmen) enters as (3, 3). Leaving: (5, 3) in the update 0x11b begins, (3, 3) from the next and it stays (3, 3) (the writer of 5 and 3 is the vehicle's input provider, not the player states, so this is VEH-5's to confirm).

**Leave.** The same X press while seated (edge), with the leave test of 0x322aa4: refused when the vehicle entity's word +0xc has bit 4, when record +0x31c is above 0, or when record +0x311 bit 6 (VehicleAllowJumpOut 0) is set. Oracle: in `m11-5-in-donut-van` (jump out denied by the mission script) X, B and Y did nothing; in `in-vehicle` and with the police car X leaves. Jump Out begin 0x3222f4: when the state was 0x11c the player node is placed at the seat; role 3 plays; the start position goes to ctrl+0x1f8; the **exit position** (stored at ctrl+0x268) is the first of eight candidates with floor below: the angles 90, 270, 0, 180, 45, 135, 225, 315 degrees (table 0x357bfc), local offset (max(2.0, 1.0 + half x) * sin a, 0, max(2.0, 1.0 + half z) * cos a) transformed by the vehicle node (0x254250), a line test from the vehicle (0x22b108), a floor ray 12.0 (when 0x229e6c(vehicle) is 1) or 4.0 below (0x21ab20) and a lift loop of 0.25 steps (0x2d8da0); without a candidate the player lands on top of the vehicle (box centre + half y + player half y, 0x3225a8). Message 85 to the vehicle and **PlayerVehicleExiting** (`*0x375fe8`) are sent in the begin. Update 0x322778 follows the same lerp and arc from seat to exit, without heading change; the event function 0x3222cc pushes state 1 and 0x32273c puts the node on the exit position. Oracle: van_dulmen at node (613.369, 17.972, 1062.516) with forward (-0.1995, 0, 0.9795): jump out from the seat (613.369, 17.772, 1062.516), 22 updates, exit (615.329, 16.604, 1062.915) = 2.0 m to the vehicle's local +x; worker FP_Vehicle_03: exit (941.95, 16.77, 1115.64) = 2.0 m to the local +x. Police car, player teleported 1.0 m from the box on the local +x side: exit (885.96, 16.62, 1118.72) = node + 2.0 * local +x. Presses during the jump in (0.5 s and 0.7 s after it began) did nothing; a press two updates after Drive began left at once (no lockout). While a mission script holds the player's controls X does nothing: in the police car of `m11-2-patrol-car` the entering fires `1-1_Release_Car_Script` (director camera, the dialog "I've marked the location of the delivery van on your map, Officer.", then the hint about A and B), and X pressed 3 s after Drive began was ignored in two runs; about 8 s after it left at once.
