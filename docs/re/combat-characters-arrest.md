# Combat: the arrest of a character

The player's arrest of a level character lying arrestable after a takedown: the A tap of the player's attack input 0x1fdbd4, the arrest test 0x2107e0, the player's state ARRESTING and the target's states ARREST and ARRESTED with the event CharacterArrested. Split from [combat-characters.md](combat-characters.md), which has the takedown that makes a character arrestable. Items: [combat-characters.md](../plans/features/combat-characters.md) (feature file).

### The A tap

- 0x1bb690 counts the held updates of ctrl+0xc bit 0x80 (A sets 0x84 while held) in the byte 0x374512, as for Y: a release after 1 to 9 held updates sets ctrl+0xe bit 0x80. With ctrl+0x315 bit 0 the bit 0x80 of ctrl+0xc and the counter are cleared instead.
- 0x1fdbd4 (the player's attack input, reached from the ground action handler 0x20b984): when the Y tap (ctrl+0xe bit 0x20) takes no branch, an A tap (0x210b50 mode 0, mask 0x80) outside state 0x101 calls 0x21a1d0 (it returns the player's range ctrl+0x80 in s0) and the search 0x219c08(player, 1, 0, 0, 1, 9, 0x1b7358, 0) with the range ctrl+0x80 and the angle *0x372e4c. The fifth argument 1 (r8) skips every candidate that is not a character (0x245b00, at 0x219d6c) and the special targets after the near list (0x219f24, 0x21a058). The bad-guy all-around angle (180) comes from the second argument r1 (its stack copy, 0x219e40), which both the A and the Y searches set.
- A target found is kept when the grab reach test 0x19bd98(player, target) or the arrest test 0x2107e0(target) passes or it is a boss (+0xc bit 14); else ctrl+0x140 is cleared. A target in ARREST or ARRESTED is cleared (0x1fe070).
- Later in the same function (0x1fe774) the A tap with nothing carried (ctrl+0x13c 0) and a kept target: with 0x2107e0 the player's ctrl+0x130 = target, 0x2414b4(player, 322 ARRESTING), the target's ctrl+0x130 = player and 0x2414b4(target, 323 ARREST). Without it, unless the player's 0x23d610 refuses, the grab paths: 0x19bfc0 (ability 50 or a boss), 0x1d38fc (ability 51) or 0x16bf8c (not read).
- 0x2107e0(target): 0 when 0x21e0d4 holds, sub +0x122 (arrest health) is not 0, or 0x23cff4(*0x376000) finds a CharacterAvoidArrest trigger; then 1 only in the states 0x123, 0x125, 0x127, 0x129, 0x12b (jump table 0x210848), 0x12d, 0x12f, 0x134, 0x135 and 0x13c with a non-zero arrest damage (sheet 2 byte +10 of the row of the combat record byte +0xe). These are the stun states whose enter lowers the arrest health ([combat-characters.md §2026-11-02T15:37:50](combat-characters.md#ts-20261102-153750)).
- 0x21e0d4 (a character paired with another): 1 in 227..231, 254..260, 262..273, 419, 420, and in 163 / 164 when the attack record byte +9 is 4..10.
- Other callers of 0x2107e0 (0x2b84a8, 0x2c4790) are AI updates that go idle (0x2246c0) when their target ctrl+0xc4 is a character in ARREST, ARRESTED or arrestable.
