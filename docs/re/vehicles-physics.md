## 2026-10-15T12:21:00+02:00 — Physics body, part 1: the rigid body, gravity and damping (REA Ghidra 12.1.2, llvm-objdump, Azahar GDB memory traces at fixed dt 0.033)

Evidence: REA pseudo code of `artifacts/game/game.arm.elf` for 0x33ce74 (ev_f74cd22e…), 0x33c520 (ev_255f45f8…), 0x243f90 (ev_118ae5f5…), 0x243e9c (ev_e5f04ec4…), 0x1f2c20 (ev_15c6356d…), 0x1f2eb4 (ev_571aa437…), 0x1f2a50 (ev_ef53e44b…), 0x2d47dc (ev_330e7b4e…), 0x30c768 (ev_10b4043c…), 0x1f3124 (ev_eadcc1b8…); llvm-objdump for 0x33aea8 (vehicle body update), 0x2d41c8, 0x2d4254, 0x30c630. Oracle: states `drv-police-car-seated` and `m11-5-in-donut-van` (New 3DS, OpenGL, port 24736), `ctrace 0x1571dc` for the entities, `read` of records and bodies, `dt 0.033 0.033`, `hold A`, `memtrace` of the rigid body (0x1d8 bytes) and the first 0x100 bytes of the vehicle body, outputs under `artifacts/oracle/agentVBODY-out/` (idle, drive, coast, vidle, vdrive; not committed).

**Two objects.** `entity+0x84` is the vehicle body (0xdd4 bytes, vtable 0x365910, constructor 0x33ce74). Its field `+0x7c` points to a generic rigid body (about 0x200 bytes, the physics world's body class), which holds the state. The constructor only sets defaults; 0x33c520 reads the node matrix and position into the rigid body, sets its damping (0x1e124c, 0.02 and 0.02, clamped to 0..1, written to rigid `+0x1c0` and `+0x1c4`), clears the velocities and sets `+0xe4` = 0. The class loader 0x243f90 stores `DampingLinear` and `DampingAngular` in the vehicle body (`+0xd58`, `+0xd5c`), `TopSpeed` (`+0x2a0`), `Mass` into the rigid body through 0x1f3124 (inverse mass `+0x15c`, inverse inertia `+0x190..+0x198`), `CentreOfMass`, `InertiaScale`. 0x243e9c stores the engine values (Friction, IdleSpeed, Inertia, MaxRPM, Redline, dtPower).

**Rigid body layout (read from the trace, matches the code).** `+0x04/+0x14/+0x24` matrix rows, `+0x34` position (x, y, y up, z), `+0x44` predicted matrix, `+0x84` copy of the velocities, `+0xcc` flags (bit 0 and 1: no dynamics), `+0xd8` mode (2 and 5 are skipped by the world's gravity pass 0x30c630 and by the force, damping and predict pass of 0x30c768; the vehicle update 0x33aea8 returns early for them), `+0x13c` linear velocity, `+0x14c` angular velocity, `+0x15c` inverse mass, `+0x160` gravity scale (1, 1, 1), `+0x170` gravity force (= `+0x180` times the mass), `+0x180` gravity vector, `+0x1a0` force, `+0x1b0` torque, `+0x1c0/+0x1c4` damping, `+0x1c8` sleep switch (0 here).

**World step 0x30c768 (read).** A fixed-step loop (the leftover time is kept in world `+0xf0`). Per step: virtual 0x8c (gravity pass 0x30c630: every body whose mode is not 2 or 5 and whose flags `+0xcc & 3` are zero gets force += gravity force times the scale, 0x2d41c8), the characters' updates, virtual 0x90 and 0x94, then for every body with `(+0xe8 & 2)` and `(+0xcc & 0x103) == 0` and mode not 2 or 5 (unless world byte `+0x58` is set): 0x1f2eb4 applies the forces (velocity += force times inverse mass times dt; angular velocity += inverse world inertia (`+0x10c..+0x134`) times torque times dt, then clamped to 90 degrees per step), 0x1f2c20 damps (velocity *= `pow(1 - damping, dt)`, angular the same; the sleep branch with `+0x1c8` is read, not used), 0x2d4ad4 = 0x1f2a50 predicts the next matrix (position += velocity times dt; the orientation quaternion advances by half dt times the angular velocity and is normalised) into `+0x44`. Then the collision pass, the constraint solver (0x30cdf8), and 0x2d47dc commits `+0x44` into `+0x04` and copies the velocities to `+0x84`.

**Measured (oracle).**
- Gravity: rigid `+0x180` = (0, -39, 0) in the police trooper (Mass 1300 in the class file, inverse mass 1/2600) and in the donut van (Mass 2000, inverse mass 1/4000), `+0x170` = (0, -101400, 0) and (0, -155999.98, 0): the mass in the rigid body is twice the class `Mass`, gravity is 3 times the class `Gravity` -13. Where the factors come from is not read (the class loader does not name `Gravity`; the only string reader 0x2c85f4 is another class).
- Modes: both vehicles are mode 2 while parked or seated without input, and become mode 1 (dynamic) when A is held (trooper: 12 updates after the hold started, the van: immediately). In mode 2 the body does not move at all (position unchanged over 8 updates).
- Linear damping: the damping `+0x1c0` of an update equals `DampingLinear * (0.04 * |v|)^2` with the speed `|v|` of the update before (0x33b0d4..0x33b120: 0x2536a8 of the velocity vector, capped at 25.0 (compare at 0x33b0f0 with the constant at 0x33b1f8), scaled by 0.04, squared, times `+0xd58`); 8 trace rows from 5.9 m/s to 19.1 m/s match to 6 digits (trooper `+0xd58` = 0.02, `+0xd5c` = 0.1, `+0x2a0` TopSpeed 28). Angular damping is the constant `DampingAngular`.
- The vertical velocity of a driven vehicle is small (the van climbs a slope at +0.1 to +1.4 m/s over 50 updates with the node height 52.74 to 53.53), the suspension and wheel contacts hold the body; gravity alone cannot be isolated from a drive.
- Over 50 updates the world velocity is about the body speed (`+0xc0`, forward speed) but the position per update is not exactly velocity times dt (0.0588 against 0.0583 at 1.77 m/s): the constraint pass changes the velocity after the prediction.

**Port.** `src/runtime/vehicle_body.*`: gravity, the damping formula and the vertical integration (force, damping, position in the order above); `src/game_vehicle.cpp` gives each moving vehicle a vertical fall state and settles it on the probe ground with the stand-in contact (VEH-S20). Orientation, angular velocity, the solver and the wheels stay out (VEH-30..32).

Unknowns:

- The fixed step of the world (0x30c768 argument 2, a vtable call) and the largest number of steps per update; the trace used a fixed game dt 0.033.
- What switches mode 2 to 1 (the update 0x33aea8 calls 0x2308e4(rigid, 0) after 0x33b44c when the controls or the speed demand it; the trooper needed 12 updates of A).
- The quaternion convention of 0x1eb950 (matrix to quaternion) used by the prediction.
- Everything after the prediction: the collision pass and the constraint solver 0x30cdf8, the wheel ray casts and suspension (0x33b378..0x33cdfc, 0x33ba70, 0x33bb08, 0x33c718), the engine and gear model (the rest of 0x33aea8 from 0x33b564, the table at 0x376d74).
