## 2026-10-15T12:21:00+02:00 — Physics body, part 1: the rigid body, gravity and damping (REA Ghidra 12.1.2, llvm-objdump, Azahar GDB memory traces at fixed dt 0.033)

Evidence: REA pseudo code of `artifacts/game/game.arm.elf` for 0x33ce74 (ev_f74cd22e…), 0x33c520 (ev_255f45f8…), 0x243f90 (ev_118ae5f5…), 0x243e9c (ev_e5f04ec4…), 0x1f2c20 (ev_15c6356d…), 0x1f2eb4 (ev_571aa437…), 0x1f2a50 (ev_ef53e44b…), 0x2d47dc (ev_330e7b4e…), 0x30c768 (ev_10b4043c…), 0x1f3124 (ev_eadcc1b8…); llvm-objdump for 0x33aea8 (vehicle body update), 0x2d41c8, 0x2d4254, 0x30c630. Oracle: states `drv-police-car-seated` and `m11-5-in-donut-van` (New 3DS, OpenGL, port 24736), `ctrace 0x1571dc` for the entities, `read` of records and bodies, `dt 0.033 0.033`, `hold A`, `memtrace` of the rigid body (0x1d8 bytes) and the first 0x100 bytes of the vehicle body, outputs under `artifacts/oracle/agentVBODY-out/` (idle, drive, coast, vidle, vdrive; not committed).

**Two objects.** `entity+0x84` is the vehicle body (0xdd4 bytes, vtable 0x365910, constructor 0x33ce74). Its field `+0x7c` points to a generic rigid body (about 0x200 bytes, the physics world's body class), which holds the state. The constructor only sets defaults; 0x33c520 reads the node matrix and position into the rigid body, sets its damping (0x1e124c, 0.02 and 0.02, clamped to 0..1, written to rigid `+0x1c0` and `+0x1c4`), clears the velocities and sets `+0xe4` = 0. The class loader 0x243f90 stores `DampingLinear` and `DampingAngular` in the vehicle body (`+0xd58`, `+0xd5c`), `TopSpeed` (`+0x2a0`), `Mass` into the rigid body through 0x1f3124 (inverse mass `+0x15c`, inverse inertia `+0x190..+0x198`), `CentreOfMass`, `InertiaScale`. 0x243e9c stores the engine values (Friction, IdleSpeed, Inertia, MaxRPM, Redline, dtPower).

**Rigid body layout (read from the trace, matches the code).** `+0x04/+0x14/+0x24` matrix rows, `+0x34` position (x, y, y up, z), `+0x44` predicted matrix, `+0x84` copy of the velocities, `+0xcc` flags (bit 0 and 1: no dynamics), `+0xd8` mode (2 and 5 are skipped by the world's gravity pass 0x30c630 and by the force, damping and predict pass of 0x30c768; the vehicle update 0x33aea8 returns early for them), `+0x13c` linear velocity, `+0x14c` angular velocity, `+0x15c` inverse mass, `+0x160` gravity scale (1, 1, 1), `+0x170` gravity force (= `+0x180` times the mass), `+0x180` gravity vector, `+0x1a0` force, `+0x1b0` torque, `+0x1c0/+0x1c4` damping, `+0x1c8` sleep switch (0 here).

**World step 0x30c768 (read).** A fixed-step loop (the leftover time is kept in world `+0xf0`). Per step: virtual 0x8c (gravity pass 0x30c630: every body whose mode is not 2 or 5 and whose flags `+0xcc & 3` are zero gets force += gravity force times the scale, 0x2d41c8), the characters' updates, virtual 0x90 and 0x94, then for every body with `(+0xe8 & 2)` and `(+0xcc & 0x103) == 0` and mode not 2 or 5 (unless world byte `+0x58` is set): 0x1f2eb4 applies the forces (velocity += force times inverse mass times dt; angular velocity += inverse world inertia (`+0x10c..+0x134`) times torque times dt, then clamped to 90 degrees per step), 0x1f2c20 damps (velocity *= `pow(1 - damping, dt)`, angular the same; the sleep branch with `+0x1c8` is read, not used), 0x2d4ad4 = 0x1f2a50 predicts the next matrix (position += velocity times dt; the orientation quaternion advances by half dt times the angular velocity and is normalised) into `+0x44`. Then the collision pass, the constraint solver (0x30cdf8), and 0x2d47dc commits `+0x44` into `+0x04` and copies the velocities to `+0x84`.

**Measured (oracle).**
- Gravity: rigid `+0x180` = (0, -39, 0) in the police trooper (Mass 1300 in the class file, inverse mass 1/2600) and in the donut van (Mass 2000, inverse mass 1/4000), `+0x170` = (0, -101400, 0) and (0, -155999.98, 0): the mass in the rigid body is twice the class `Mass`, gravity is 3 times the class `Gravity` -13. Where the factors come from is not read (the class loader does not name `Gravity`; the only string reader 0x2c85f4 is another class).
- Modes: both vehicles are mode 2 while parked or seated without input, and become mode 1 (dynamic) when A is held (trooper: 12 updates after the hold started, the van: immediately). In mode 2 the body does not move at all (position unchanged over 8 updates).
- Linear damping: the damping `+0x1c0` of an update equals `DampingLinear * (0.04 * |v|)^2` with the speed `|v|` of the update before (0x33b0d4..0x33b120: 0x2536a8 of the velocity vector, capped at 25.0 (compare at 0x33b0f0 with the constant at 0x33b1f8), scaled by 0.04, squared, times `+0xd58`); 8 trace rows from 5.9 m/s to 19.1 m/s match to 6 digits (trooper `+0xd58` = 0.02, `+0xd5c` = 0.1, `+0x2a0` TopSpeed 28). Angular damping is the constant `DampingAngular`.
- The vertical velocity of a driven vehicle is small (the van climbs a slope at +0.1 to +1.4 m/s over 50 updates with the node height 52.74 to 53.53), the suspension and wheel contacts hold the body; gravity alone cannot be isolated from a drive.
- Over 50 updates the world velocity is about the body speed (`+0xc0`, forward speed) but the position per update is not exactly velocity times dt (0.0588 against 0.0583 at 1.77 m/s): the constraint pass changes the velocity after the prediction.

**Port.** `src/runtime/vehicle_body.*`: gravity, the damping formula and the vertical integration (force, damping, position in the order above); `src/game_vehicle.cpp` gives each moving vehicle a vertical fall state and settles it on the probe ground with the stand-in contact (VEH-S20). Orientation, angular velocity, the solver and the wheels stay out (VEH-30..32).

Unknowns:
- Why the rigid body mass is twice `Mass` and gravity three times `Gravity` (class loader 0x243f90 does not touch gravity; candidates: the world setup or the vehicle link 0x156f74), and whether other classes differ.
- The fixed step of the world (0x30c768 argument 2, a vtable call) and the largest number of steps per update; the trace used a fixed game dt 0.033.
- What switches mode 2 to 1 (the update 0x33aea8 calls 0x2308e4(rigid, 0) after 0x33b44c when the controls or the speed demand it; the trooper needed 12 updates of A).
- The quaternion convention of 0x1eb950 (matrix to quaternion) used by the prediction.
- Everything after the prediction: the collision pass and the constraint solver 0x30cdf8, the wheel ray casts and suspension (0x33b378..0x33cdfc, 0x33ba70, 0x33bb08, 0x33c718), the engine and gear model (the rest of 0x33aea8 from 0x33b564, the table at 0x376d74).

## 2026-10-15T13:41:02+02:00 — Physics body, part 2: orientation, wheels and suspension (llvm-objdump, REA Ghidra 12.1.2, Azahar GDB memory traces at fixed dt 0.033)

Evidence: llvm-objdump of `artifacts/game/game.arm.elf` for the vehicle update 0x33aea8, the wheel functions 0x3396cc, 0x339030, 0x339a30, 0x339374, 0x339538, the wheel constructor 0x339bec, the contact pass 0x33c718, the force at a point 0x33a4b4 / 0x2d40f0, the point velocity 0x1e131c / 0x341694, 0x1f2eb4, the ray test 0x155888 (start only); REA pseudo code of 0x243f90 (ev_118ae5f5…), 0x243628 (ev_0016e965…), 0x1f2a50, 0x2d47dc, 0x1f2c20 (batch ev_57bd6fa1…). Oracle (own port 24737, no RPC, no pokes): states `drv-police-car-seated` (trooper entity 0x08a88440, body 0x08e16100, rigid 0x08e16f40) and `m11-5-in-donut-van` (van entity 0x08a7c480 from the player's ctrl+0x130, body 0x08e12880, rigid 0x08e136c0); `memtrace` of the body (0xdd4 bytes), the rigid body (0x1d8), the four wheel records (body+0x2b8, 0x550 bytes) and the suspension settings at 0x376e58 (0xb0 bytes), 45 updates with A held then 150 released. Outputs `artifacts/oracle/agentVPHYS-out/` (`body0.mem`, `vbody0.mem`, `pdrive.mem`, `pcoast.mem`, `vdrive.mem`, `vcoast.mem`; not committed).

**Two matrix conventions.** The physics world keeps R with the body axes as columns; 0x232788 transposes it into the vehicle body (+0x04 rows right, up, forward), which is the node convention. Checked in the trace: body rows equal the rigid columns, and the inverse world inertia `+0x10c..+0x134` equals R diag(+0x190) Rᵀ (the off-diagonal xz term has the sign of that product). 0x1f2a50 converts the matrix to a quaternion (x, y, z, w) with 0x1eb950, adds dt/2 (ω, 0) ⊗ q (Hamilton product, ω in world axes), normalises and writes R back; together with the world inertia this is a consistent world-frame rigid body.

**Rigid body step (completes part 1).** 0x1f2eb4: velocity += force × inverse mass × dt; angular velocity += Iw⁻¹ × torque × dt; when |ω| dt exceeds π/2 (0x3fc90fdb) ω is scaled to it. 0x2d40f0 (called by 0x33a4b4 with the point minus `+0x34`): force += F times the linear factor `+0x160` (1, 1, 1), torque += arm × F times the angular factor `+0x22c`. 0x341694: velocity + ω × arm. Damping `+0x1c4` is the class `DampingAngular` (0.1 in both vehicles), `+0x1c8` (sleep branch of 0x1f2c20) is 0.

**Loaders.** 0x243f90 stores `WheelSuspensionScale` (body+0xd9c: trooper 1, van 0.75), `CentreOfMass` at body+0x50 and its negative at body+0x44 (the node offset), `GFXOffset`, `CollSpheres`, `CollRadius`, `DropOffset`, and gets the inertia from the collision shape (virtual +0x20 with the mass), scales it by `InertiaScale` and passes it to 0x1f3124 (body+0xa0 = inertia: trooper 7212.7 / 18590 / 2812.2, van 18144 / 28098 / 20787; the shape function is not read). 0x243628 (per wheel n, "Front" for n & 2 == 0, else "Rear" at 0x243a5c): ratio = 2 × `WheelSuspensionRatio` (front) or 2 − 2 × ratio (rear); w+0xf0 = `%sShockDampingNeg`, w+0xec = `%sShockDampingPos`, w+0xe8 = `%sShockStiffness`, each × ratio × body+0x84 (2.0 in both vehicles); w+0xe4 = `%sShockTravelMax`; w+0x50 = (wheel node x, `%sShockYoffset`, wheel node z) − CentreOfMass (0x227790 subtracts); w+0x44 = `%sWheelWidth` / 4, negative for a negative x; w+0x40 = `%sWheelRadius`; also skid width, wheel inertia, friction, drift, hand brake and brake values (VEH-32). The port's reader matches the oracle records of both vehicles (trooper wheel 0: mount (−0.85, 0.0685, 1.52), side −0.07825, radius 0.48, travel 1, stiffness 18.2, damping 5.2 / 3.9; rear 18.2, 2.8 / 2.1; van wheel 0: (−1.1, −0.3564, 1.32), −0.1, 0.45, 0.9, 41.6, 15.6 / 7.8; rear 28, 8.4 / 4.2).

**Wheel update in 0x33aea8 (dynamic modes only).** With rigid +0xbc set: per wheel 0x3396cc (steer and spin angles; wheel matrix = local (mount + side offset in x) × body; ray start w+0x100 = wheel centre; w+0x118 = max(travel − radius, 0.001) + radius), then 0x33c718, then per wheel 0x339a30 (0x339030 suspension, wheel spin). 0x33c718: the ray starts w+0x150 = 0.99 (constructor 0x339bec) above the centre along the body's up row and ends w+0x118 below it; 0x23b5e8 picks a triangle and 0x155888 tests it: the start must be on the front side of the triangle plane (record normal and d) and the end behind it; a hit stores the point (w+0x120), the record normal (w+0x12c) and the surface byte; a hit above the centre is moved to the centre; then bodies within 5 units (0x2e2f48) are swept with 0x1ea6a0.

**Suspension 0x339030 (with contact).** distance = |centre − hit| (w+0xe0); c = clamp((travel − distance) / travel, 0, 1); two 11-entry tables at 0x376eac and 0x376ed8 are read at c × 10 with linear interpolation (0x1e12e8). Force (w+0xb4) = w+0xe8 × WheelSuspensionScale × table1(c) × (setting+4 × 4000) × 3 − damping × WheelSuspensionScale × v_n × table2(c) × (setting+8 × 2000) × 0.9, at least 0, where v_n is the rigid point velocity at the wheel centre along the hit normal and damping is w+0xec when v_n ≥ 0 (extending) and w+0xf0 when compressing. The force along the normal is applied at the wheel centre, only for wheels 0..3 (w+0x74 < 4). Without contact the force is 0 and w+0xe0 the ray length. The settings at 0x376e58 are 1, 0.75, 0.7, 2.5, ... and both tables are written at run time (the ELF data differs): table1 = c^2.5, table2 = max(2 − 2 c^2.5, 0.25) at the 11 points.

**Measured.** The formula reproduces w+0xb4 of every wheel in the clean updates of the traces (trooper updates 2..5: 26846.994 against 26846.995, 20173.477 against 20173.476 extending, 29894.326 against 29894.330 compressing; van updates 2..5 within 0.01); updates where the solver or a kerb changes the state differ by up to 4 %. Under acceleration the trooper pitches nose-up by up to 0.7 degrees and settles; braking pitches it down to −1.2 degrees (tyre forces at the contact, VEH-32). The trooper came to rest on the street and slept (mode 2) at rigid y 17.0445 with wheel distances 0.5288 front and 0.5263 rear (formula equilibrium on flat ground 0.5291). The parked trooper of `drv-police-car-seated` is not at rest (forces sum 105295 against the weight 101400): it has not been simulated since it was placed.

**Port.** `src/runtime/vehicle_body.*` (rigid body with quaternion, inverse inertia, force at a point, point velocity, step), `src/runtime/vehicle_wheels.*` (tables, ray, suspension, the vehicle step), the reader in `src/runtime/vehicle.cpp`; `src/game_vehicle.cpp` runs it for the driven or moving vehicle. Stand-ins: the kinematic engine and tyres set the horizontal velocity and heading, the inertia is the measured one, the body's ground contact replaces the collision pass and solver ([features/vehicles-physics.md](../plans/features/vehicles-physics.md)). A vehicle PositionObject (`LevelSession::placeVehicle`, [script-natives-flags.md §2026-10-15T13:42](script-natives-flags.md#ts-20261015-134200)) rebuilds the rigid body at the target, so its velocities start at 0; whether the original clears them is not read.
