# Script natives: the death of a character (message 1 of kinds 27, 121 and 242)

What DestroyProp / KillObject (and every other caller of the entity destroy 0x23cd80) does to a character: message 1 of the character handler 0x18e4c8, the fall-apart body 0x1d3b50 and its pool, the bricks, the stud drop and the respawn tail. Split from [script-natives-world.md](script-natives-world.md). For developers; no game assets.

## Current state

- The tail: the player's entity, every entity while 0x22b0b4 holds, and a character with `AIFriendly` and `AIRespawnIfFriendly` (default 1) come back: enabled with the node hidden, in locomotion state 11 (DEAD); any other character is disabled and goes to the after-death 0x21e218 (back to its placement or `AIMillAbout` 0x22471c for friendly ones, else out of the engaged table 0x58deb0 / 0x58dec8 through 0x21decc). - A character that comes back: DEAD's enter 0x1ab8d4 zeroes the spawn counter +0x2ce and runs 0x21e218 for non-players; 0x225f5c counts it to 136; above 64 DEAD gives way to IDLE (with the event *0x3775ec for friendly respawns), from 66 the node is shown and pulses red to white until 136. - The fall-apart body: a pool of four slots (0x374208, 152 bytes each) with the part objects BrokenHead / Body / Arm / Legs / Hips 01..04 of `levels/charbits.blvl` (the head is a dummy that carries the character's head node; the legs or the hips by a draw modulo 2); 32 updates of flight (gravity 0.075 per update, bounce 0.5, a line test of the part's bounds sphere, rest below speed 2.0 with x and z below 0.125), then 32 updates of blinking on bit 0 of 0x241098, then free; sound 548 at the start. [§2026-10-19T13:21:05](#ts-20261019-132105)
- Open: the player's DEAD branch, 0x21e700, 0x22471c and the other AI helpers of 0x21e218, the order of 0x225f5c and the state update, the character sound table behind record +0x2c2, the unit of the clock 0x241098, the texture copy 0x270ae0 / 0x22ad0c and the head attach 0x22690c / 0x2268f4, the writer of 0x5163e8 + 0x25 and of byte +0x12e bit 5.
