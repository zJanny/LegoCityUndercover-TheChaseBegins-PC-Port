# Super bricks (placed pickups and the counter)

The super build bricks that lie in a level (the pickup classes `pickups/pickup_sbbrick2x2` and `pickups/pickup_sbbrick2x4`, the rainbow bricks), how one is picked up and the counter it feeds. The bricks that fly out of destroyed objects are [debris.md](debris.md) (VEH-123). Replica: `SuperBrickField` (src/runtime/super_brick.*), `SuperBrickObject` / `drawableObjects` (src/assets/level.cpp), glue src/game_super_bricks.*. Studs: [studs.md](studs.md).
