## 2026-10-15T09:30:35+02:00 — Driving: player input, state rules, state writers, forced stop, stuck rule and the oracle measurements of the police trooper (VEH-5)

Sources: REA (Ghidra 12.1.2) pseudo code of `artifacts/game/game.arm.elf` for 0x29cd9c (ev_8aef62e0…), 0x16ce9c (ev_84d99f42…), 0x23019c (ev_66902c00…), 0x1571dc (ev_b447bd1a…), 0x307abc (ev_e77730f8…) and 0x16cccc (ev_693f115e…); `llvm-objdump` for the vehicle message handler (0x159bd0, 0x179850..0x17a648), 0x2424d0 and 0x24257c. Oracle: Azahar (New 3DS, OpenGL, `--rpc --rpc-input`, port 24721, home `agentDRV`), state `drv-police-car-seated`, made from `m11-2-patrol-car` as measuring aids: the police car entity (0x08a88440, record 0x08a884e0, found by scanning the heap for the record) had its locked bit cleared by an RPC write, the player was teleported 1.9 m beside it (car position + right axis) and X was pressed; the user's state files were not modified. The sampler `tools/oracle/vehicle_drive_sampler.py` (RPC reads of the clock 0x376b0c, the vehicle node matrix (rows of 16 bytes from node+0x5c, translation node+0x8c), the record states and the body inputs) wrote one row per changed position; pad input through the pad and stick caves of `--rpc-input`. Raw samples: `artifacts/oracle/agentDRV-out/*.csv` (pcA, pcB, brk, rev, coast, exitmv, forced, both, revsteer, hiL, hiH, pcSt*, pcS1; not committed). Tests: `vehicle-drive` (pins the numbers below), `game-vehicle-drive`.

**Update timing.** The game runs 30 updates per second: the clock 0x376b0c advances by about 100 ticks per update (3000 per second) and the vehicle node moves once per update. Speeds below are node movement along the vehicle's forward axis per update times 30.

**Player input 0x29cd9c (read).** Called by the vehicle update 0x1571dc as the input provider when `0x24255c(*0x3772b8)` is this vehicle (state 0x11c of the player), else 0x1d8df0 (AI), 0x26d934 when towed. It reads the pad device (`*0x3769e4`, channels of 20 bytes from `+0x14`) with the channel numbers stored in words (read by RPC): 0x3769f0 = 14, 0x3769f4 = 15, 0x376a00 = 16, 0x376a04 = 17, 0x376a0c = 19. Steer (record +0x288) = channel 0 (the circle pad x value after its dead zone: raw 50 gives 0, 60 gives 0.1005, 78 gives 0.291, 100 gives 0.5238, 120 gives 0.7354, 140 gives 0.9471, 156 gives 1.0; equal to body+0x1bc), replaced by -1 when channel 14 is not 0 and by +1 when channel 15 is not 0 (channel 15 wins); accelerate (+0x28c) = 1 when channel 16 (A) is above 0; brake (+0x290) = 1 when channel 17 (B) is above 0; both are set to 0 when the float at record +0x14 is above 0.75 (field not identified, 0 in the oracle). Channel 19 (Y) edge: the horn (sound id from the vehicle table, 0x2e4 when `0x2369a8(7)`). Inputs are cleared at the end of every `0x23019c`, so the provider writes them each update.

**Input copy 0x23019c (read).** Per update, called from the post step 0x16ce9c when the requested state equals the current one (0x2424d0 skips the post step in an update where +4 differs from +2): state 3 sets the hand brake input (+0x294) = 1; states 4 and 0xb set it as well; 6 and 7 leave the inputs alone; every other state (5 included) has no hand brake. A forced stop (record +0x310 not 0) sets hand brake 1, accelerate 0, brake 1, keeps the steering, and sets brake 0 again when the body speed (0x229948) is 0.1 or less (or the body flag at +0x294 is 0). The four values are copied to body+0x1bc (steer), +0x1c0 (accelerate), +0x1c4 (brake), +0x1c8 (hand brake) and +0x1cc = 0, then the record inputs are cleared. It also runs the engine sound machine and starts the `MusicTrack` for the player's vehicle in state 4. Oracle check of the forced stop: record +0x310 poked to 1 at 18.07 m/s with A held: 11.39 m/s after 2 updates, 8.86 after 3, 7.32 after 4, 5.49 after 5, 3.89 after 6, 0.65 after 8, at rest after 10 (run `forced`).

**Writers of the record states (read, oracle).** The class message handler 0x159bd0 passes messages it does not know to the base handler (pointer *0x584d30); the vehicle messages are handled at 0x179850..0x17a648 (r5 vehicle entity, r4 record, r7 sender). Message 84 (sent by the Drive begin 0x307abc through 0x244fc8): when the sender's controller byte +0xd8 has bit 4 clear the forced stop byte +0x310 is cleared (the oracle shows it stays 1 for the player, so the bit is set for the player); with the music flag (+0x312 bit 2) the track starts (0x2a39f0 or 0x1821bc); **when the state +2 is not 6 or 7 the requested state +4 becomes 5** (store at 0x17a4cc, lr 0x17a4d8; a GDB write watch on the record +4 during the entering of the oracle police car stopped once there); then 0x29f014 runs and clears record +0x311 bit 2 when it returns 0. Message 85 (the jump out): **when +2 is not 10 and not 4 the requested state becomes 3** (0x17a530..0x17a544), then 0x22e6ec, 0x2b429c and the light and prompt flags. Message 83 (before the jump in): clears the link flag of the entity at +0x2a4 when the sender is the player and sends the message 0x80000007 to the vehicle. Message 1 (0x16cccc) is the destroy path: +4 = 6, the vehicle node gets flag 0x20 and the wheels get random offsets. The copy of the requested state to the current one: a GDB write watch on record +2 stopped once while the update 0x1571dc was inside 0x24257c (the call at 0x157270, lr 0x157274); that function was read only in part (it also computes the on-screen flag +0x312 bit 3), so the place of the store is not confirmed. Vehicle state meanings found: 3 hand brake (after the player jumped out), 4 parked (hand brake), 5 driven by the player, 6 and 7 destroyed, 8 stuck, 10 unshovable, 11 hand brake.

**Forced stop in the oracle state.** The mission script VanBreakdown sets +0x310 to 1; in `drv-police-car-seated` (the player sits in the car) it was still 1, the body inputs showed the hand brake, and it became 0 in the first update after A was pressed: a GDB write watch on +0x310 stopped at 0x2b9e24, inside the native SetForcedStopOnMyVehicle (0x2b9df0), called with 0 by a script (the state was saved while the mission script that follows the entering ran; the script was not identified).

**Stuck rule 0x16ce9c (read).** After 0x23019c: when the float at (node matrix)+0x14 (compared as an integer with 0x3e4ccccd = 0.2) is below 0.2 and the entity flag word +0xc has bit 4 clear, and the body speed (0x230174) is below 2.0, the timer record +0x31c adds dt (0x23602c); above 2.0 seconds +4 becomes 8. Otherwise (not tipped over, or frozen) the timer is 0; a tipped vehicle that is fast keeps it. The leave test 0x322aa4 refuses the jump out while +0x31c is above 0. Ported as `updateStuckTimer`; the stand-in body never tips over, so it never fires.

**Measured numbers (police trooper, class value TopSpeed 28).** All from free-running samples with one stick or button state (RPC, no stepping), speeds in m/s, k in updates:
- A held from standstill, speed after k updates counted from the first update that moved (median of five runs, spread 0.2 to 0.3): k 1: 2.1 (the first update jumps to 2.1 to 2.7), 5: 3.44, 10: 4.86, 20: 8.57, 30: 12.73, 40: 16.54, 50: 19.84, 60: 22.8, 70: 25.1, 80: 26.45; it ends at 27.3 to 28.6 (the class TopSpeed 28). Distance after 30 updates 7.04 to 7.09, after 60 updates 24.3 to 25.5.
- B (brake) pressed while moving forward: both runs lose 11.8 m/s in the first three updates (18.24 to 6.47 in run `pcB`, 25.66 to 13.83 in run `brk`), then 1.0 to 1.2 per update (30 m/s^2) until rest (`pcB`: 3.41 after 6 updates, 0.88 after 8; `brk`: 8.17 after 6, 4.0 after 10, 0.42 after 14).
- B at rest (or after the stop) reverses: speed -4.2 after 30 updates, -6.5 after 45, -7.83 after 60 (3.9 m/s^2; run `rev`, the car hit something at -9.9). Reverse with A pressed while moving backward was not measured. A and B together at 18.4 m/s brake like B alone and then reverse (7.06 after 3 updates, 1.35 after 8, -0.4 after 10; run `both`).
- Neither button: 20.0 m/s gives 19.19 after 2 updates, 17.87 after 6, 16.19 after 10, 12.77 after 20, 9.33 after 30, 5.98 after 40, rest after 50 (about 10.5 m/s^2, run `coast`); moving backward the speed falls 3.4 to 5.5 m/s^2. Under about 1.7 m/s the car stops within two updates.
- Hand brake (state 3 after the jump out, run `exitmv`, X pressed at 16 m/s while A was held): 8.86 after 2 updates, 4.91 after 6, 2.87 after 8, 0.12 after 14, then the record is (3, 3).
- Steering with A held from standstill, yaw rate (heading = atan2(forward x, forward z), a positive stick turns it up, the car turns right) at 8.6 m/s after 20 updates: steer 0.291: 0.093 rad/s, 0.5238: 0.253, 0.7354: 0.635, 0.9471: 0.93, 1.0: 1.007; steer 0.1005 at 19 m/s: 0.047. Curvature (yaw rate / speed) at 8.7 m/s: 0.01075, 0.02905, 0.0732, 0.116, 0.1207 for those steer values, below 8 m/s the same, 0.93 of it at 11.6, 0.84 at 15.6, 0.80 at 17, about 0.57 at 24 (runs `pcSt*`, `pcS1`, `hiL`, `hiH`; full lock at 23 to 25 m/s reaches 1.2 to 1.5 rad/s over one second, the yaw rate builds up over 0.2 s at low speed). Reversing with steer right turns the heading down (run `revsteer`: the sign of the yaw rate follows the sign of the speed). Without a stick the car did not turn on the flat street in front of the station (yaw rates below 0.001); on a road with a camber the traffic van of `in-vehicle` drifted at 0.1 to 0.18 rad/s, which the stand-in does not have.
