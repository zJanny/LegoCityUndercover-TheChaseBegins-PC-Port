The contact step passes the car's driver +0x58 and entity +0x5c (+ extra) as box, max 128, mode 1, the car itself as excluded owner ([traffic-ai-driver-steering.md §2026-11-07T12:12:03](traffic-ai-driver-steering.md#ts-20261107-121203)).

**Enable.** The grid's word +0x6030 (the product of the three cell counts) is 0: return 0.

**Cells.** low = centre - half and high = centre + half (0x257f14, 0x23f50c). 0x20ea60 gives the cell of a point: per axis floor((p - origin) x inverse cell) with the origin at +0x6020 (x, y, z), the inverse cell size at +0x6014 and the counts at +0x602c (x), +0x602d (y), +0x602e (z); the value is converted to an integer and clamped: at least count - 1 gives count - 1, above 0 gives itself, else 0. The cell index is (y x count z + z) x count x + x (the cell table at +0x6034, 4 bytes per cell: byte 0 the entry count, hword 2 the start in the u16 index list at +0x2000 of the grid; the indices go through the entry table at +0).

**Walk.** y from the low to the high cell is the outer loop, z the middle one, x the inner one; in each cell the entries in list order. The list index is a running total: an entry is stored at list[total + cell count], only while max - total (taken at the start of the cell) is above the cell's own count; otherwise the rest of that cell is skipped, and later cells add nothing either. Every stored entry gets bit 1 of its byte +5 (the visited mark, also tested first: an entry with bit 0 or 1 of +5 is skipped); after the walk the mark of every listed entry is cleared again. The return value is the number listed.

**Filters of one entry, in this order.** (entry +0 = mask word) mask & include is not 0; mask & exclude is 0; (1 << kind) (kind = byte +4, a shift of 32 or more gives 0) & kinds include is not 0 and & kinds exclude is 0; the owner (entry +0x10) is not the excluded owner; with owner hword +0x10 include not 0: hword & include not 0; hword & exclude is 0; the owner's node (owner +0x3c) word +0 has no bit 0x20; with owner word +0xc include not 0: word & include not 0; word & exclude is 0. For mode 1 (0x238168) the entry's matrix is the owner's node matrix (0x250810), times the entry's extra matrix +0x14 when that is not null (0x2588f4(out, extra, node): out = extra x node, rows of 4 floats, the last column (0, 0, 0, 1)); 0x22ec3c(matrix, shape +0x18, out) makes the world box of the shape (centre at +0, half at +0xc): the centre goes through the matrix (0x250da8: x' = (y m10 + z m20) + (m30 + x m00), likewise y and z), the half extents stay when m00 and m11 as integers are above 0x3f7fbe77, else each axis i is |m0i hx| + |m1i hy| + |m2i hz|. 0x2335e4(query centre, query half, box centre, box half) then tests |box centre - query centre| <= query half + box half on every axis. Mode 2 (0x2381c8) does a different test through 0x2397a0 (not read); every other mode accepts the entry after the filters.

**The cells of an entry** are fixed by the cell update 0x209cb8 (called by the add 0x1f5cd0 and when the owner moves): the same world box (0x22ec3c of the owner's node matrix times +0x14 and the shape), its low and high corner through 0x20ea60 and the stored cell bounds at entry +8..+0xd; when they differ the entry leaves its old cells and joins the new ones.

**The grid build (0x2713e4..0x271750).** The level load takes the minimum and maximum over the level's bounds, stores the extents at +8, +0xc and +0x10, the inverse sizes at +0x14, +0x18 and +0x1c (1 / extent, with an adjustment at 0x2716b4..0x2716f4 that is not read), the origin at +0x20, +0x24, +0x28 and the counts as floor(extent x inverse + 0.999) per axis (literal 0x3f7ff972); the cell table has count-product entries of 4 bytes (the initial fill at 0x271724). The count product must stay below 2048 (else a table is allocated at 0x2716f8). The adjustments and the extents' sources are not read.

**What the port takes.** `gatherTrafficGrid` follows the walk, filters, box test and cap above, with the descriptor of `traffic_driver_steering.hpp` (mode 1) and the excluded owner passed by the hook. The mark of bit 1 is a local flag per call. An entry's cells are those of its current world box (the cell update has always run), and an entry's place in a cell's list is its registration order: STAND-IN TRAFFIC-S39. The port's levels have no grid; the layout of `TrafficGrid` is an input (TRAFFIC-52), and chase-game passes no characters to the contact step yet (TRAFFIC-53).
