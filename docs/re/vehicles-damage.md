**Closing speed 0x19b5c4 (with 0x19b648).** The speed is the dot product of the contact normal with the relative velocity of the contact point. For a manifold point (the message's +8 is 0): v + w x arm of the vehicle's rigid body with the arm the point's local coordinates as stored (+0x00 for body A, +0x10 for B; not turned into world axes), minus the other body's velocity at its arm (the other point when the other entity has a body); the normal is the point's normal on B (+0x40), negated for side 0. For a floor contact (+8 is the record): the arm is the contact point minus the body position, the normal is the record's (+0x14), negated for side 0, no other body. A negative result is a speed away from the contact; the thresholds below compare the float bits as signed integers.
