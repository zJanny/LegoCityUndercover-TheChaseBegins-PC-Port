**The launch 0x28abd4(from, to, out, s0 speed, s1 gravity).** d = to - from; (dx, dz) is normalised in place by 0x23ba14 (length sqrt(dx dx + dz dz), (0, 1) when the length's bits are at most 0x34000000), h its length. tangent = dy / h + (gravity h) / ((speed speed) x 2.0). When the tangent's bits are above 0x3f800000 (above 1.0): if dy is below h, speed = sqrt(((gravity h) h) / ((h - dy) x 2.0)); either way the tangent becomes 1.0. out = (dx speed, speed tangent, dz speed).
