# Traffic: the vehicle body of a driven car

Subtopic of [traffic.md](traffic.md); the AI driver that moves the car's node is in [traffic-ai-driver.md](traffic-ai-driver.md), its run on the cars in [traffic-driver-connect.md](traffic-driver-connect.md). This file: what the vehicle physics body of a traffic car does while the driver moves the node (the kinematic switch, the body commands, the world update, the body step), which step puts an entity on the update list and its body into the physics world, and who reads the floor cell the spawn writes. Feature files: [pedestrians-traffic-cars-driver.md](../plans/features/pedestrians-traffic-cars-driver.md), [vehicles-physics-collision.md](../plans/features/vehicles-physics-collision.md), [pedestrians-traffic.md](../plans/features/pedestrians-traffic.md).

## Current state

- A traffic car's rigid body is kinematic (rigid flags bit 1, mass 0, collision group 2, mask 61) and does not move by itself. Before every world step the world update 0x2e2bc8 copies the node into each kinematic body of the world (0x20cc30: the node's rotation, the origin at the node's image of the centre of mass). The driver's route mover stores the velocity. The body step 0x33aea8 then only tracks the floor cell and spins the wheels by forward speed / radius. [§2026-11-12T00:10:13](#ts-20261112-001013)
- The kinematic switch 0x213c74 and the world membership 0x243300 only queue commands (0x1fb648); the queue runs at the start of the next world update (0x1ed6a8 → 0x2e3390), so the rigid flags change one update after the switch. [§2026-11-12T00:10:13](#ts-20261112-001013)
- An entity is on the update list 0x580564 while 0x23c1a8(entity, room) finds bit 1 of the room layer hword for its class and its room's type, the entity enabled and its byte +0x13 = 0. Joining sends message 0x80000004 (a vehicle puts its body into the physics world), leaving sends 0x80000003 (out again). 0x23c1a8 runs when an entity is enabled, disabled or moved to another room, and the room apply 0x189c90 runs the same test for every entity of a room whose state or type byte changed. [§2026-11-12T00:10:13](#ts-20261112-001013)
- The globals 0x3745b4 and 0x3745b0 are constants: the static initialiser 0x34498c stores 2.0 (the mass scale of the player's vehicle) and 6.0 (the mass scale of a car the chase start puts into driver state 5). [§2026-11-12T00:10:13](#ts-20261112-001013)
- The floor cell at body +0x8c is the body's current cell of the floor graph. Its readers are the cell tracker 0x20c0e4 (walks on from it every world step and at every placement 0x23091c), the floor pass and the wheel ray. The traffic spawn writes the spawn point's cell so that the tracker of the placement that follows walks instead of searching; with −1 the tracker searches with 0x1edaac at the body's position. [§2026-11-12T00:10:13](#ts-20261112-001013)
- Port: `src/runtime/traffic_car_body.*` (test `traffic-car-body`), `src/runtime/entity_update_list.*` (test `entity-update-list`), glue `src/game_traffic_bodies.cpp`. A kinematic traffic body does not collide with the player's car yet. [§2026-11-12T00:10:13](#ts-20261112-001013)

## 2026-11-12T00:10:13+01:00 — The kinematic body of a traffic car, the update list and its messages, the mass scale constants and the readers of the body's floor cell (llvm-objdump, no REA session; one servant measurement)

Method: `llvm-objdump -d --triple=armv6k-none-eabi --mattr=+vfp2` on `artifacts/game/game.arm.elf`, words read from the ELF segments (worker 20261111-chase-A, 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-12); no REA session and no emulator of its own. One measurement is quoted from the emulator servant of the same round (20261111-chase-servant, 2026-11-11, RPC reads, three loads that agree, recorded from a save state: mission 1-4, the player on the showroom roof). Functions read: 0x213c74, 0x243300, 0x2432d8, 0x23f0fc, 0x1fb648 (head), 0x2e3390 (commands 0, 1, 3, 4, 6), 0x1e3ccc, 0x242518, 0x2308e4, 0x229920, 0x24092c, 0x229948, 0x20cc30, 0x2429fc, 0x218e5c, 0x33c434, 0x2e2bc8, 0x2e394c (head), 0x33aea8 (0x33aea8..0x33b0d0), 0x20c0e4, 0x16ce9c, 0x173534, 0x22d480, 0x22d5f0, 0x23c1a8, 0x189c90 (0x189ef8..0x18a074), 0x242cf0, 0x229bf0, the message branches 0x17a618 and 0x17a648, the spawn 0x2df330..0x2df4e4, the placement 0x23091c (head and tail), the static initialiser 0x34498c, 0x213dc8 and its callers 0x1afda0, 0x2f7090, 0x3200bc, the mover's test 0x261614..0x26162c. Port: `src/runtime/traffic_car_body.*`, `src/runtime/entity_update_list.*`, tests `traffic-car-body`, `entity-update-list`.

**The kinematic switch 0x213c74(body, on)** (called by 0x22a270(entity, on) with the entity's body +0x84).
- On: gravity 0 (0x242518 → rigid gravity (0, 0, 0)), rigid activation state +0xd8 = 4 (0x1e3ccc), per wheel w+0xe0 = w+0x54 − w+0x60 + w+0x40 (mount y, the y of the vector at w+0x5c, radius), body +0xc4 = (+0xc4 & ~0x8000) | 0x20. Then, only when rigid +0xcc has bit 1 clear: the body takes the node's transform (vtable +0x18 = 0x33c434 with the entity's node) and command 4 is queued (0x2432d8).
- Off, only when body +0xc4 has bit 0x20: activation state 1, gravity = the global 0x376c40 (−39.0), bit 0x20 cleared, command 3 queued (0x23f0fc).

**The body command queue.** 0x1fb648(world 0x5863b4, rigid, command, 1, value) appends a 12-byte entry (rigid, command byte, flag byte, a float) to the world's queue under a lock; at 512 entries it runs the queue first (0x1ed6a8). 0x1ed6a8 runs every entry through 0x2e3390 and is called at the start of the world update 0x2e2bc8. So a switch takes effect in the rigid flags at the next world update. The commands of 0x2e3390 (jump table 0x2e33b0):
- 0 (0x2e33d8, queued by 0x243300 with 1): rigid +0xcc bit 0x80 cleared, the rigid body added to the Bullet world (world vtable +0x88 with the body's hwords +0x76 group and +0x78 mask). A body with +0xc4 bit 0 whose rigid flags have neither bit 0 nor bit 1 gets the global gravity. A body whose hword +0x9c is 37 gets group 64 and +0xc4 bit 0x100.
- 1 (0x2e34fc, 0x243300 with 0): removed from the world (vtable +0x54), +0xcc bit 0x80 set.
- 3 (0x2e3670, dynamic): only with +0xcc bit 0 or 1. Removed when in the world, bits 0 and 1 cleared, the mass body +0xc8 and inertia +0xa0 set again (0x1f3124), mask 63 (0xffff with body +0xc4 bit 0x40), group 1, added again when in the world.
- 4 (0x2e3760, kinematic): only with +0xcc bit 1 clear. Removed when in the world, mass 0 and inertia 0, +0xcc = (+0xcc & ~1) | 2, mask 61, group 2, added again when in the world.
- 6 (0x2e3534, queued by the mass scale 0x213dc8): mass and inertia times body +0x84, the wheels' shock values times it.

The rigid flags that follow are the measured ones: 0x100 from the body build, 0x102 for a live traffic car and 0x182 for one out of the world ([traffic-ai-driver.md §2026-10-25T12:26:09](traffic-ai-driver.md#ts-20261025-122609), [vehicles-physics-props.md §2026-10-16T09:53:49](vehicles-physics-props.md#ts-20261016-095349)).

**The world membership 0x243300(body, on).** Body byte +0x90: bit 0 = wanted in the world, bit 1 = the rigid body is built. On: with bit 0 clear and bit 1 set, command 0; bit 0 set. Off: with both bits set, command 1; bit 0 cleared. The vehicle message handler calls it for message 0x80000004 with 1 unless the entity's hword +0x10 has bit 0 (0x17a618), and for 0x80000003 with 0 (0x17a684) unless the entity is the vehicle the player is entering (0x2b9d9c(player) = entity, 0x24255c(player) ≠ entity, +0x10 bit 5 clear: 0x17a698, not read).

**Other small calls.** 0x2308e4(rigid, force): with force 0 nothing for a rigid body with flag bit 0 or 1; else activation state 1 unless it is 4 or 5, and the deactivation time +0xdc = 0. 0x229920(body, v): rigid +0x13c..+0x144 = v, +0x148 = 0. 0x24092c(body) returns rigid +0x13c..+0x144; 0x229948(body) is its dot product with body +0x24 (the forward row). The route mover tests rigid +0xcc bit 1 (`tst #2` at 0x26161c) before 0x229920.

**The node copy 0x20cc30(body, node).** M = the node's world matrix (0x2429d0) with row 3 replaced by the image of body +0x50 (the centre of mass) under it (0x250da8). 0x2429fc(body, M) writes the transposed rows (0x218e5c) to the rigid transform +4..+0x40, to the interpolation transform +0x44..+0x80 and to the record at rigid +0x1e4 when set, and M itself to body +4. The vehicle's vtable +0x18 (0x33c434) calls it and then re-poses each wheel node (0x33929c).

**The world update 0x2e2bc8(world, dt)**, called from 0x2e55fc:
1. The queue runs (0x1ed6a8).
2. The world's entity list is rebuilt (count world +0x3058, entities +0x305c, positions +0x3124): every collision object of the Bullet world whose body has +0xc4 bit 0 and whose entity hword +0x10 has neither bit 0 nor bit 5.
3. Every collision object whose activation state is neither 2 nor 5, that has a body and an entity without +0x10 bits 0 and 5 and whose rigid flags have bit 1 takes its node: 0x20cc30(body, entity node). This is how a kinematic body follows the node the driver moved.
4. The Bullet world steps (world vtable +0x30 with dt and the step count).
5. 0x2e394c: for the same objects without rigid bit 1 (dynamic), the node is set from the body (from the record at rigid +0x1e4; not read further).
6. Every rigid body of the world list that is not static and whose entity lacks +0x10 bits 0 and 5 gets body +4 = its transform (transposed back, w = 1).

**The body step 0x33aea8(body, dt)** as far as a kinematic car goes: body +4 = the rigid transform; body +0xc0 = the velocity (0x24092c) along the forward row; then the cell tracker 0x20c0e4 when the byte 0x376c35 is set (it is 1 in the data image; all 17 literal references load its address to read it), else the empty 0x33a91c. Then:
- rigid +0xcc bit 1 set (kinematic, 0x33afc4..0x33b084): per wheel w+0x88 = +0xc0 / (w+0x40 × 2π) × 2π (literal 0x33b1e8 = 6.2831855), w+0x80 += w+0x88 × dt; the wheel node (w+0x7c) gets the local matrix identity, turned by π (literal 0x33b1ec) about y and spun the other way when w+0x50 (the mount x) is negative, turned by w+0x80 about x (0x2354b4), with the node's local position kept. Nothing else: no forces, no wheel rays, body +0xda0 untouched.
- else body +0xc4 bit 0x20 set (the switch asked, the queue has not run): body +0xda0 = 4 and return.
- else the dynamic step ([vehicles-physics.md](vehicles-physics.md)).

**The vehicle post step 0x16ce9c after the driver's post step.** For an AI car body +0xc4 gets bit 0x100000 (cleared for the others). When the entity's +0x10 lacks bit 5: a kinematic body whose node moved since the start of the function (0x24ef70 > 0), or a dynamic one with a velocity above 0, runs the prop smash 0x275c00 in the record states (+2) 2, 3, 5, 8 and 9; in the states 4, 6 and 7 only a dynamic body does. So a driven traffic car smashes props while it moves.

**The update list (VEH-91).** The list is the array 0x580564 of 8-byte entries (entity, priority byte) with its count at 0x376aa0 and a dirty byte at 0x376a9c, guarded by the lock *0x376b20; the entity's hword +0x1a is its index, 0xffff when it is not listed.
- 0x173534(entity): a listed entity only gets its entry's priority set from entity byte +0x19 (dirty when it changed). Otherwise, when the type row (0x583124 + 40 × byte +0x12) has a function at +0xc, the entity is appended, +0x1a = its index, and it gets message 0x80000004 (0x244fc8); before that, with +0x10 bit 0 clear, 0x22d5f0(entity +0x40, 0).
- 0x22d480(entity): nothing when +0x1a is 0xffff. Else the last entry takes the entity's place (its +0x1a is updated), +0x1a = 0xffff, 0x22d5f0(entity +0x40, 1), and when byte +0x20 of the type record (entity +0x20) is not 0, message 0x80000003.
- 0x23c1a8(entity, room): without a room, 0x22d480. Else the hword at *0x376ad4 + 4 + 2 × (class × 17 + min(room +0x3a, 16)) with class = entity byte +0x18: bit 0 picks the room node the entity's node hangs under; with bit 1 set, +0x10 bit 0 clear and byte +0x13 = 0 it ends in 0x173534, else in 0x22d480. This extends [cutscene-actors-props.md §2026-10-19T13:47:29](cutscene-actors-props.md#ts-20261019-134729), which left both ends unread.
- Callers of 0x23c1a8 (12): among them the room change of an entity 0x229bf0(entity, room) (it links the entity into the room's list +0x58 + 8 × byte +0x13, or +0x70 when disabled, then calls it) and the twin inside the enable 0x242cf0 → 0x242b08 (which looks the room up first). The body placement 0x23091c calls 0x229bf0 when the room's node differs from the node's parent.
- The room apply 0x189c90 (step 4 of the scene room update, [streaming-rooms.md](streaming-rooms.md)): when the room's state byte +0x39 or type byte +0x3a differs from the new request, it walks the four entity lists of the room (+0x58, +0x60, +0x68, +0x70) and runs the body of 0x23c1a8 inline for every entity (0x189f64..0x18a074).
- So the step that activates a vehicle is: its enable (0x242cf0), its move into a room (0x229bf0), or the room apply when its room's type changes to one whose layer hword has bit 1 for the vehicle's class. For a traffic car (class 0 in the default room of type 0, [traffic.md §2026-10-25T12:35:43](traffic.md#ts-20261025-123543)) the spawn's enable puts it on the list and its body into the world, and the removal's disable takes both out.
- Servant measurement that agrees: of the 16 junction entities of lvl_01 (type byte +0x12 = 192, class 2, +0x13 = 0, priority 128), the 15 in the scene's default room (type byte 0) are on the list; the one in a loaded room that is not a neighbour of the current room (room state 2, type byte 255, entity +0x10 = 0x40) is not. The list held 76 or 77 entries. The hword +0x1a of an entity can be stale after the list was sorted, so membership was tested by scanning. Room types then: 0 for the current room, 1 for its eight neighbours, 255 for all others. Class 2 has bit 1 for the types 0 to 15 only.

**The mass scale globals.** No instruction stores to 0x3745b4 or 0x3745b0 through a literal of their own. The static initialiser 0x34498c stores nine floats at 0x3745a4 (literal 0x3449d4): 0.5, 0.15, 30.0, 6.0, 2.0, 0.8, 132.0, 3.5, 1.0. So 0x3745b0 = 6.0 and 0x3745b4 = 2.0 (the data image holds 0 there). 0x3745b4 is read at 0x2f7090 and 0x3200bc for 0x213dc8 of the player's vehicle, which agrees with the measured 2 ([vehicles-physics-props.md §2026-10-16T09:53:49](vehicles-physics-props.md#ts-20261016-095349)); 0x3745b0 is read at 0x1afda0, where a car made dynamic (0x22a270(entity, 0)) gets record request +4 = 2, mass scale 6.0 and driver request +0x79 = 5.

**The floor cell of the body (PREGEN-7).** Body +0x8c is the body's current cell in the level's floor graph and +0x1a4 the position it was tracked at.
- The tracker 0x20c0e4(body): nothing without a floor graph (*0x376c68 = 0). With +0x8c < 0: +0x1a4 = the rigid position, +0x8c = 0x1edaac(+0x1a4). Else +0x8c = 0x23b5e8(+0x8c, +0x1a4, position, 0, 0) (the walk, which fails at the graph's edge) and +0x1a4 = the position. It returns whether the cell is valid.
- Its callers: the body step 0x33aea8 every world step, kinematic or not; the placement 0x23091c at its end; 0x2f1dd0; the floor pass.
- The other readers are the floor pass and the wheel ray of a dynamic body ([vehicles-physics-collision.md](vehicles-physics-collision.md), [vehicles-physics-props.md §2026-10-16T09:53:49](vehicles-physics-props.md#ts-20261016-095349)).
- The spawn 0x2def8c writes the point's cell to +0x8c (0x2df3c8), takes the transform from the placed node (vtable +0x18) and calls the placement 0x23091c with the rigid transform, whose tracker then walks from the body's last position +0x1a4 (where the car was removed, or its level position) with the cell just written. A point without a cell writes −1, and the tracker searches at the body's position, which is the point raised by the spawn's height offset. What the walk returns for a last position far from the cell is not measured.

**Port.** `traffic_car_body.*` holds the body's fields, the switch, the membership, the commands 0, 1, 3 and 4, the wake, the velocity, the node copy with its test, the body step up to the dynamic branch and the spawn's part; `TrafficCarBodies` gives the hooks (`TrafficBodyHooks`) the driver and the manager use. `traffic_driver_connect` calls the switch, tests the kinematic bit for the mover and stores the mover's velocity. `entity_update_list.*` holds 0x23c1a8, 0x173534 and 0x22d480. In `chase-game` each traffic car has such a body: the spawn seeds it, the room test puts it into the world (one log line per change), the node is copied before the world steps and the body step runs per world step. Stand-in TRAFFIC-S29 lists what the glue chooses.
