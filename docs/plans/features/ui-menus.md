# UI menus and screens

## 1. Scope

Every UI screen of the game that is not the HUD, the script text screens or the start screens: the pause screen, the bottom-screen round menu and its pages (options, languages, credits, shop, award viewer, code entry, item grids), the map buttons, the character change screen, photo mode and the screens drawn by code. The census of all 55 layouts and their owners is [ui-screens.md](../../re/ui-screens.md); the HUD is [hud-ui.md](hud-ui.md), the text screens [hud-ui-text.md](hud-ui-text.md), the start screens and slot list [front-end.md](front-end.md). Bottom-screen menus and UI are used by clicking them with the mouse (the touch screen), not with extra keyboard keys; pad buttons the original reads keep their bindings ([input.md](input.md)).

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-10-30T14:19:06+01:00. Start during gameplay opens the pause screen of the original in `chase-game`: PauseTop on the top screen and the round menu (Resume, Options, Credits, Exit) with the Back and Select shortcuts on the bottom screen, drawn from their layouts with the original's storyboards. The D-pad (or the circle pad through 0x2289b8), taps and swipes with the mouse on the bottom screen turn and select; Resume, B and Start close it and the game goes on. Options opens the Options page: Up / Down change the sound effects and music volume, Invert camera toggles, B saves a change and goes back; the Language row opens the list of the nine languages (drawn as the round menu), A on one stores it in the options and saves; Credits and Exit do nothing yet; the top screen's counts are not drawn. The award viewer is in [ui-menus-award-viewer.md](ui-menus-award-viewer.md). Every other menu is not ported. The map buttons of the bottom screen run in `chase-game`: the characters and scan buttons slide in as in the original, a click acts on the first update of a touch inside the original's click areas, a short click on the characters button is a tap (not ported further) and a hold of more than 0.5 s opens the dark characters panel without its portraits; the fast travel pad opens the fast travel page (exit button, six slots) and its exit button closes it. The other buttons' actions are logged as not ported.

## 3. Verified behaviour

- Opening: Start in a free gameplay update pauses, the next update opens the screen and the game stops updating; closing waits for TransitionOff_Round (0.5 s), then the game goes on (`game-pause-menu`, `pause-menu`): [ui-screens-pause.md §2026-10-19T20:34](../../re/ui-screens-pause.md#ts-20261019-203404).
- Page 0 entries, texts, pictures, sounds (0x14 select, 0x11 back and close, 0x12 turn) and the order of page manager, round menu and Start test from the code; slot u shows item (selected + u - move - 2) mod 4 (`pause-menu`).
- Touch: taps within 32 pixels of the three button centres and swipes (more than 30 pixels over 4 updates, 15 updates apart) from 0x1f08d4; the centres are computed from the layout and equal the oracle's reads (54, 119), (159, 119), (264, 119) (`pause-menu-real-data`). A left click that starts on the bottom screen is a touch, not A.
- Options page: rows, texts, Up / Down steps (the music step previewed through the sound effects byte), A, B, the Left / Right reload of the sound effects byte and the Select shortcut per row from the code, checked against the oracle (20261028-uiport-B-1) (`options-page`, `pause-menu`, `game-pause-menu`): [ui-screens-pause.md §2026-10-28T22:51:42](../../re/ui-screens-pause.md#ts-20261028-225142).
- Picture of the open menu against the oracle (r38-B-1): 7.0 top and 4.3 bottom mean difference, 0.9 on the bottom without the text rows; six sample pixels pinned in `pause-menu-real-data`.
