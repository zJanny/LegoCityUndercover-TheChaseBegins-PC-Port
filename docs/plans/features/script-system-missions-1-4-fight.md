# Script system: missions, mission 1-4, the showroom fight (AI spawners, StartState, the end of the first fight)

## 1. Scope

The fight in the car showroom of mission 1-4: the AI spawner class (kind 14) that holds and releases the goons, the StartState entries their spawn applies and the end of the first fight through the arrest. What an idle goon does next (the decision of AI state 0, the sleep, the return to the spawn point) is the subtopic [script-system-missions-1-4-conduct](script-system-missions-1-4-conduct.md). Split from [script-system-missions-1-4](script-system-missions-1-4.md), which keeps the gap list and ranking of the mission. The player's attack, the hit reaction, the takedown and the arrest themselves are [combat-characters](combat-characters.md) (COMBAT-*) and PLAYER-26 to PLAYER-29.

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-11-07T12:17:10+01:00. The AI spawner class runs for all kind-14 objects: the 17 characters of the eight spawners of the mission are held (hidden) from level load, the arrival's TriggerObject releases goons 03 and 06 at their placements, and a spawner fires its `TriggerObject` reference once all its characters are done. Each character that comes out gets the spawn word of its spawner's StartState entry: goons 03 and 06 (StartState 0, no Runto) go idle and stand, the goons of Spawners 06, 07 and 01 (StartState 6) super-jump to their `1-4_AI_Goon_NN_GoTo`. Each goon can be thrown twice by Y taps, which raises CharacterArrestable (for goons 03 and 06 `1-4_Character_Arrestable_Script` runs), and arrested by A, which raises CharacterArrested and makes the goon done for its spawner: arresting goons 03 and 06 fires `1-4_SO_Phase1`, which starts `1-4_Spawner_06`. What the released goons do next (the idle decision, the sighting into AI state 2, the return to the spawn point) is in [script-system-missions-1-4-conduct](script-system-missions-1-4-conduct.md); the fight state itself is not ported (COMBAT-9). The counter throws and the grab are not ported (COMBAT-10). Next: the fight state (COMBAT-9), the spawner details (SCRIPT-419).
