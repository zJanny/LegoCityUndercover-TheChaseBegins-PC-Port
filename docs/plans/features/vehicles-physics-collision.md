# Vehicles: physics body collision

## 1. Scope

The vehicle body's collision in the physics world: the floor collision pass 0x1c54f8 on the floor graph, the sleep rule, and the Bullet contacts with the level's static boxes (compound child box, box-box detector, manifolds, sequential impulse solver). Split from [vehicles-physics.md](vehicles-physics.md) (rigid body, wheels, engine, tyres). The contacts between vehicle bodies are here too; kinematic objects, placed objects and damage are VEH-7 in [vehicles.md](vehicles.md).

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-10-15T20:27+02:00. The body collides with the level floor through the original's floor pass and sleeps by the original's rule (VEH-40, VEH-53 closed), with the 217 static Bullet boxes (VEH-60, VEH-63 closed) and with the other vehicle bodies through the original's pair contacts and two-body solve; parked vehicles are sleeping bodies that a hit wakes. The floor pass has the second cell search (an awake car teleported under an overhead floor layer is no longer thrown, VEH-94). Still missing: the sphere sweep of the floor pass (VEH-61), the compound shape of the donut van (VEH-62), details of the Bullet contacts (VEH-70, VEH-90, VEH-91).
