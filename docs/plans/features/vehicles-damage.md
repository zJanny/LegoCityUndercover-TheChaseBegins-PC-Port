# Vehicles: contact damage and the break

## 1. Scope

What a hit does to the vehicle the player drives: the contact message 0x80000002 and the damage message 0 of the vehicle handler 0x1797c4, the hearts (+0x30d) and health points (+0x30c) of the vehicle record, the cool-downs, the brick burst of a contact, and the break of the car (message 1, 0x16cccc, the wreck state). Split from [vehicles.md](vehicles.md) (VEH-7, VEH-93, VEH-99). The physics contacts that send the message are in [vehicles-physics-collision.md](vehicles-physics-collision.md).

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-10-18T14:47+02:00. The player's vehicle takes damage from contacts as the original does: the closing speed of every Bullet manifold point (static boxes and other vehicles) and of every floor-pass contact is computed (0x19b5c4), a contact above 20 m/s costs one heart per 0.5 s (a hit of 27.9 m/s took the oracle trooper from 5 to 4 hearts, as the port does), the HUD shows the hearts. A car whose hearts reach 0, or that is pushed against an obstacle for 3 s (the stuck timer of the driven body), breaks: the body leaves the world, the wheels fly and bounce, the player stays seated, and 2 s later the car is back with full hearts at its last good place, as in the oracle trace. A contact above 10 m/s bursts six bricks that fly, bounce, rest and vanish as in the oracle traces; a lost heart flashes the car white and red and starts the smoke emitter; the break bursts bricks too. Super bricks, the sounds and the fade of the return are missing.

## 3. Verified behaviour

- Damage rules (`src/runtime/vehicle_damage.*`, tests `vehicle-damage`, `vehicle-box-contact`): speed thresholds 10 / 20 / 60, `(min(speed, 60) - 20) x 0.025 x 5` split into whole hearts and a fraction, the points take `floor(fraction x 100)`, one heart per message that cannot take the points, at most one heart for a contact message, 0.5 s cool-down, 1.0 s brick cool-down, the hearts are the vehicle table's health (trooper 5). Values are from the disassembly; the first hit of the oracle run (hearts 5 to 4, +0x304 = 0.5, +0x308 = 1.0, health byte back at 100) agrees ([vehicles-damage.md §2026-10-16T10:21:53](../../re/vehicles-damage.md#ts-20261016-102153)).
- The pair rule of the callback (flag word bit 0 / 1 for a driven body, the wheel rule, `BumpCollision` friction 0.2 and restitution 1.1; `applyPairContactRule`, test `vehicle-damage`) replaces the earlier flag 2 stand-in; the recorded pair hit (`vehicle-world-real-data`) still holds.
- The break and the return (`src/runtime/vehicle_break.*`, tests `vehicle-break`, `game-vehicle-break`): the stuck timer (3 s below 3 m/s with one pedal, decay 0.25, zero above 5) and its type 6 message with 1000 hearts, the launch vectors of 0x16cccc, the wheel step 0x1d27f4 (fall 0.075, bounce 0.5, rest rule, spin), state 7 after 2.0 s, the return in one update to state 5 with the table hearts at the oldest entry of the matrix ring of seven; the wheel rows, the 61 updates of state 6 and the record values of the return agree with the oracle trace ([vehicles-damage.md §2026-10-18T09:34:10](../../re/vehicles-damage.md#ts-20261018-093410)).
- Bricks, flash and smoke (`src/runtime/debris.*`, `stepVehicleFlash`, `vehicleSmokePosition`; tests `debris-core`, `vehicle-damage`): the burst 0x229fb8 / 0x21e2e4 / 0x19913c, the record update 0x180168 and object placement 0x28a4b0, the flash counter 0x18cca0 with the white materials of 0x230ae8, the smoke 0x22a124 (0x11e / 0x11f). Eleven oracle bricks of a van contact and a lamp post replay exactly ([debris.md §2026-10-18T14:46:04](../../re/debris.md#ts-20261018-144604)); the flash counter runs 1 to 15 as in the round 16 trace.
- The processed-callback hook of Bullet's manifold refresh (`refreshManifold` observer, 0x1e518c) and the floor-pass message before the impulse (0x1c5cec) deliver the messages in the original's order.
