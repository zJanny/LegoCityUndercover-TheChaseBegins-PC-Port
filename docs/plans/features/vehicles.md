# Vehicles

## 1. Scope

Vehicle objects (class type 183, `levels/vehicles/vehicle_*`): creation and parked state, handling data, the driving update and physics, entering and leaving, the vehicle script natives, the vehicle camera, sounds and the events the mission scripts wait for. Needed for four of the eleven steps of mission 1-1 (get into `1-1_Police_Car`, drive, drive the donut van, reach the vehicle waypoints, jump out). Not covered: traffic and pursuit AI ([script-system-ai-navigation.md](script-system-ai-navigation.md)), the waypoint classes (agent work on objectives: [script-system-missions.md](script-system-missions.md)), race and RC vehicles, vehicle drops, helicopters and boats.

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-10-16T11:30+02:00. A moving vehicle smashes wobbly props that register a box (VEH-98 closed; the rest VEH-103..VEH-106). Mission 1-1 steps 7 and 8 run in `chase-game`: get into the donut van (the enter waypoint fires during the jump in), drive it to `1-1_Waypoint_05`, the script jumps the player out (VEH-9, VEH-11 closed). Vehicles of a level are created as records, drawn with their wheels, collide with the player and take the five mission 1-1 flag natives; the player can get into and out of an unlocked vehicle with the original's timing and paths (VEH-4, closed); the player can drive the police car and other vehicles with the stick, A and B on the physics body with the original's engine and tyres (VEH-5, VEH-32 closed); the camera behind the driven vehicle is the original's (VEH-8, closed; [camera-vehicle.md](../../re/camera-vehicle.md#ts-20261015-120718)); the physics body (rigid body, wheels, suspension, engine, tyres) is in [vehicles-physics.md](vehicles-physics.md); collision and sounds come with VEH-7 and VEH-10. Getting in and out: [vehicles-boarding.md](vehicles-boarding.md).
