# Script system: missions, mission 1-3 at the garage

## 1. Scope

The garage part of mission 1-3 in `chase-game`: the key the detective dirt mound reveals, its pickup, the key use at the keyhole and the garage door, the parts crate carried to the base plate and the generator. The way from the generator to the mission end is in [script-system-missions-1-3-end.md](script-system-missions-1-3-end.md). Split from [script-system-missions.md](script-system-missions.md); the roof, the super brick and the helipad of mission 1-3 are in [studs-super-bricks.md](studs-super-bricks.md) and [level-objects-buildit.md](level-objects-buildit.md), the carry classes in [level-objects-carry.md](level-objects-carry.md).

## 2. Status

The generator scene runs on the real path: A on the pull switch `1-3_Generator_Pull_Switch` (the final prop of that build-it) starts `1-3_Generator_Script`, which waits for the crane platform (103 updates, as in the oracle), stops the girder's rocking, starts the smoke of PlayParticlesGO, lets the girder guy jump down and ends with QuickSave (CTest `game-generator-1-3`); the platform and the girder do not move down, so the scene's camera shows the sky (SCRIPT-373). The girder guy is attached to the girder by the level's startup script (AttachObject), so he sways with the rocking girder and will go down with it. The script does not end the mission; the way from there to the end (push cart, crane build-it, crane switch, ledge platform) is in [script-system-missions-1-3-end.md](script-system-missions-1-3-end.md).
