# Abilities: sound scanner

## 1. Scope

The sound scanner of mission 1-2 and later missions: the `prop_scannerzoneaudio` object class, entering the scanner view, zooming and locking on a target, and the `ObjectTriggered` events that start `Scanner_Enter_Script`, `Scanner_Zoomed_Script`, `Scanner_Locked_Script` and `Scanned_Pringles` (part of SCRIPT-111 in [script-system-missions.md](script-system-missions.md)). The detective tool is in [abilities-detective.md](abilities-detective.md).

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-10-28T22:48:23+01:00. The audio zone (type 201, ScannerMode 1), the scannable objects, the player states 0x156 / 0x157 / 0x158, aim, zoom, lock, scan, exit and all zone and target triggers are ported in `chase-game`; from the roof trigger of mission 1-2 the four scanner scripts start and `EnableScanner 0` closes the scanner (test `scanner-real-data`). The overlay (frame, zoom meter, headphone icon, reticle, scan meter) is drawn from the original layouts; the per-object sound markers (green rings) and the audio / exit buttons of the map screen are drawn; the cyan wash (the material override of the audio mode, no fog) is drawn, but without the glow pass (RENDER-68); the info box of a scan (state machine, top or bottom choice, fade, icon) is drawn; the bottom screen slides the characters button out and the exit and audio buttons in at the scanner enter and back at the exit, and a click on the back button leaves the scanner like B; the sonar segments and the tip pages shown during the view are not ported (SCAN-10, SCAN-19). Real-time transition counts are ported.

## 3. Verified behaviour

- Class values of `1-2_ScannerZoneAudio` and `1-2_Dog_Prop` from the level data; settings block and audio mode values read in the oracle (the save state on the roof at the sound scanner trigger of mission 1-2).
- Real-time timing (oracle B-1): state 0x157 49 updates after the press, `EnterScanTriggerObject` 20 later, toggle and camera 56 after the entry, lock 20, scan 39, script exit 57 updates; `ZoomedInTriggerObject` every update at full zoom.
