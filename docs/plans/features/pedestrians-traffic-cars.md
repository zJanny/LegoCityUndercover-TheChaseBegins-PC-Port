# Traffic manager and the AI driver input

## 1. Scope

The traffic manager 0x527410 and the AI driver input of the spawned cars. The manager spawns and removes the level's `Traffic%02u` vehicles around the player, keeps the vehicle limit (`Traffic_MaxVehicles`, SetTrafficMaxVehicles) and fades cars in and out at `Traffic_FadeBound` boxes. The AI driver input 0x26d934 drives a spawned car along its route. Not covered: pedestrians (the pedestrian manager 0x51b33c, [pedestrians-traffic-pedestrians.md](pedestrians-traffic-pedestrians.md)), the pregen file format ([pedestrians-traffic.md](pedestrians-traffic.md)), the vehicle physics body ([vehicles-physics.md](vehicles-physics.md)), pursuit.

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-10-25T13:00:54+01:00. The manager is wired into `chase-game` by `TrafficSystem`: at the scene enter it loads the level's pregen traffic file and runs the prefill, and every update (system phase, after the pedestrians) it spawns and removes the level's `Traffic%02u` cars at pregen points around the player; EnableTrafficAndPedestrians and SetTrafficMaxVehicles reach it. The cars stand still (TRAFFIC-S7) because the AI driver is not connected to them. The routes (junction paths, EnableTrafficPath, the path follower, the route type function, the junction pairs and test) are ported as `traffic_route.*` (TRAFFIC-5) and the AI driver as `ai_driver_input`: its record, the input provider 0x26d934 with every state, the state change 0x1cb148 and the traffic post step 0x1bc79c. Not ported yet: the functions those call (route mover, steering controller, junction choice; TRAFFIC-7..9). Neither is wired to the cars.

## 3. Verified behaviour

- Read from the code (test `traffic-manager`), all from [traffic.md §2026-10-20T11:18:14](../../re/traffic.md#ts-20261020-111814):
  - the level load: the vehicle limit is min(`Traffic_MaxVehicles` default 5, car count); the spawn order is shuffled with the game's RNG; every car starts disabled with fade 255;
  - SetTrafficMaxVehicles: the argument is truncated and compared unsigned with the count;
  - the update: removal of every car while traffic is off; one spawn per update while below the limit when the countdown reaches 0, with countdown (live + live / 2) x 3; one removal per update (room, driver state, fade and distance rules); cars with owner flag 0x20 are kept;
  - the spawn: 10 tries in the leaf, the route bit, the room test, the LOD distance band (0.7 x near to 1.75 x far, 0 to far in the prefill), 16 m route spacing in x and z;
  - the 64 prefill updates of the room set-up;
  - the fade inside a `Traffic_FadeBound` box.
- Read from the code (tests `traffic-route`, `traffic-route-real-data`), all from [traffic-ai-driver.md §2026-10-25T12:19:38](../../re/traffic-ai-driver.md#ts-20261025-121938):
  - the route list: input then output paths of the junctions within 9000 of the origin (at most 32), each new one with its bit set; EnableTrafficPath and its pending list, replayed by the room set-up;
  - the route → junction pairs 0x2dfc84 and the driver's junction 0x20ff40;
  - the path follower 0x2bf2ec (nearest enabled point of the cell, below 9999, dot above 0.5 by its float bits) and the route type function (table 0x3581d4: type 0 0x1c17c0, type 1 0x1b33fc);
  - the junction test 0x2cdde4;
  - shipped levels: every traffic point lies on an open type 0 junction path; junction, route and path counts pinned.
- Read from the code, not ported: the vehicle update uses 0x26d934 for a car whose driver record has +0xaf bit 0, 0x29cd9c for the player's car and nothing for the others (0x1d8df0 is empty).
- Read from the code (test `ai-driver-input`), all from [traffic-ai-driver.md §2026-10-25T12:26:09](../../re/traffic-ai-driver.md#ts-20261025-122609):
  - the driver record of 0x1796e8: zero-filled, +0x74 = `TrafficHeightOffRoad`, +0x18 = the node matrix, +0xaf bit 0, kinematic body;
  - the state change 0x1cb148: +0x78 takes the request +0x79 with its leave and enter steps, and after a spawn the state goes 0 → 1 → 2, one update per step (the servant measured the same on a street with traffic after mission 1-1);
  - the input provider 0x26d934: the stop rule of states 2 and 3, state 5 steering 8 m ahead along the route, state 8 steering back to the path and putting the car back on it when it faces away; states 4, 6 and 7 give no input;
  - the post step 0x1bc79c: the junction step of state 3, the recovery 4 → 8, the break 6 → 7, the keep counter +0xad;
  - traffic cars are kinematic bodies (flags 0x102) except in states 4 and 6 and after a contact.

- Wired, from [traffic.md §2026-10-25T12:35:43](../../re/traffic.md#ts-20261025-123543) (tests `traffic-system`, `traffic-system-real-data`, `game-traffic`, `game-traffic-natives`):
  - a spawn places the car at the point with y raised by `TrafficHeightOffRoad` minus the centre of mass y and the point's direction (12 oracle spawns equal to four decimals); point +0x20 and driver +0x64 are the path parameter;
  - the LOD table is the pedestrian distance fade table, the ten cars of lvl_01 are LOD class 1 (spawn band 50.4 to 133, prefill 0 to 76) and have the removal radius 50; lvl_01 has ten cars and the limit 10;
  - the driver bytes after a spawn (0/1, 1/2, 2/2) and the grace byte are the ones of the state change in the entry above; the oracle shows the same.

## 4. Stand-ins

- TRAFFIC-S1 Facts the port chooses or measured on lvl_01 only: the paths of the file's points are numbered in the order of first use and every route is enabled (the route list of `traffic_route.*` is not used yet); the cars' rooms use the pedestrian room object (PED-S2); layer class 0 and the node flags (LOD class 1) are those of lvl_01's ten cars for every level; no car is pinned; the generator is the fixed-seed one of the pedestrians (PED-S6); the traffic update runs after the pedestrian update (slot order not read). needs: TRAFFIC-10, TRAFFIC-12, TRAFFIC-5. evidence: [traffic.md §2026-10-25T12:35:43](../../re/traffic.md#ts-20261025-123543)
- TRAFFIC-S2 Two cases the original leaves unread are refused by the port's own choice: an empty leaf makes the spawn fail (the original would compute random % 0), and a LOD class outside 0..2 throws (the original's -1 indexes the table one entry low). needs: TRAFFIC-3. evidence: [traffic.md §2026-10-20T11:18:14](../../re/traffic.md#ts-20261020-111814)
- TRAFFIC-S3 A route missing from the route list counts as disabled in the follower; the original tests the stored +0x24 word of the path record. Every shipped traffic point lies on a junction path (`traffic-route-real-data`). evidence: [traffic-ai-driver.md §2026-10-25T12:19:38](../../re/traffic-ai-driver.md#ts-20261025-121938)
- TRAFFIC-S4 The route list +0xc4, the pending route list and the two route → junction lists refuse a 129th entry, and a route type above 1 throws; the original writes past the lists and reads words after its table. needs: TRAFFIC-4. evidence: [traffic-ai-driver.md §2026-10-25T12:19:38](../../re/traffic-ai-driver.md#ts-20261025-121938)
- TRAFFIC-S5 The route type functions (table 0x3581d4: point, distance ahead, nearest sample), the route length, its signal object and the path follower 0x2bf2ec reach `ai_driver_input` as callbacks (`AiRouteFunctions`, labelled in the code) until the route port `traffic_route.*` is connected. needs: TRAFFIC-2. evidence: [traffic-ai-driver.md §2026-10-25T12:26:09](../../re/traffic-ai-driver.md#ts-20261025-122609)
- TRAFFIC-S7 The AI driver is not connected to the cars, so a spawned car stands still. The driver bytes go 0/1, 1/2, 2/2 one update each (state change 0x1cb148); the grace byte +0xad is set to random % 60 + 30 by the 0 -> 1 step and counts down one per update as if the car's node were always hidden (0x23de70 taken as true; the original keeps the byte at 30 or more while the node is not hidden); the speed +0x68 is 10 (8.6 to 11.3 measured); the record state is set to 1 at once and the car is placed without the kinematic switch 0x22a270 (the port makes no physics body). The state-1 draws of 0x2345dc are not made. needs: TRAFFIC-2, TRAFFIC-11. evidence: [traffic.md §2026-10-25T12:35:43](../../re/traffic.md#ts-20261025-123543)
- TRAFFIC-S8 The removal radius 0x26e130 is 50 for every car (the row comes from record +0x20, whose writer is not read; the ten cars of lvl_01 read radius 50). The LOD table 0x529e30 is no stand-in: its values are pinned in `traffic-system` (request TRAFFIC-1 of 20261025-traffic-servant). needs: TRAFFIC-12. evidence: [traffic.md §2026-10-25T12:35:43](../../re/traffic.md#ts-20261025-123543)
