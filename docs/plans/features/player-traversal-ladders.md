# Player traversal: ladders

## 1. Scope

The ladders (object kind 56: `props/prop_ladderlarge`, `props/prop_laddermedium`, `props/prop_crane_ladderlarge` and the other kind 56 classes) of [player-traversal.md](player-traversal.md): use zone, mount by the A press or by walking in, climb up and down, the clamber onto the top (state 23), the exits and their animations. Mission 1-3 climbs `1-4_Ladder01` to the roof (save state in front of the crane ladder of mission 1-3). Findings: [docs/re/player-traversal-ladders.md](../../re/player-traversal-ladders.md).
