# Player traversal: rails, climb bars and the corner rail

## 1. Scope

Rails and climb bars (object type 29) and the corner rail (type 218) of [player-traversal.md](player-traversal.md): grab from a jump, arrival, hang, shuffle, the corner onto the next rail, leaving by A or B. Findings: [docs/re/player-traversal-rails.md](../../re/player-traversal-rails.md).

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-10-18T21:37:12+02:00. Ported in `chase-game` for the route of mission 1-2 and replayed against `m12-12` (TRAV-3, TRAV-4 and TRAV-30 closed in [player-traversal.closed.md](player-traversal.closed.md)); the jump off the last climbing brick grabs the rail as in the original. The bar test also runs in the fall updates (state 5): the flag poles above the mission 1-2 trampoline are grabbed from the bounce and reached through the arrival. The other bar variants are not ported.
