# Script system: missions, mission 1-4, the roof scene and the natives of the roof and the chase

## 1. Scope

The roof scene of mission 1-4 (`1-4_SO_Phase2`, `1-4_AI_Goon_10_Run_Off_Script`, `1-4_RooftopReached`, `1-4_Jump_On_Roof`, the FallArrest bound scripts) and the four natives of the roof and the chase that had no handler (BulletTime, VehicleSetAI, VehicleSetChased, SpawnDebris2Colour). Split from [script-system-missions-1-4](script-system-missions-1-4.md), which keeps the gap list and ranking of the mission; the chase itself is SCRIPT-415 and SCRIPT-422 there.

## 4. Stand-ins

- SCRIPT-S323 The natives of the roof scene and the chase run partly as stand-ins. BulletTime steps at the start of the level update (the original's order is not read) and scales the whole level update, HUD and camera included; the flag branch (the camera shot, logged as not ported) and the mode calls of the restore are not ported (SCRIPT-431); with the flag clear the mode calls are not made. Its real clock is the scheduler's clamped period, not the original's timer, and the sound 98 state test runs only with an open audio device. VehicleSetAI and VehicleSetChased fill the traffic AI driver record of the car (no rotation in the node matrix, `TrafficHeightOffRoad` 1.0, route distance 0) and nothing steps the driver, so the stolen car does not drive. SpawnDebris2Colour starts its bricks at the object's position, without the box centre and half extent of its entity and without the original's room-flag check (it skips the burst for rooms with certain flags). needs: SCRIPT-422, SCRIPT-431. evidence: [scripts-missions-1-4.md §2026-11-02T22:40:54](../../re/scripts-missions-1-4.md#ts-20261102-224054).
- SCRIPT-S325 The class `particles` is ported only for TriggerObject's messages and the one-shot timer: the endless self-start, `ReTriggerTime` above 0 and -5, message 0xfb, `Instance` sharing, the parent node, path, damage, sound, shake, room test, the visibility delay and the objects nested in class files are not, an ended emitter counts as gone (its callback is not read) and the class update runs where the port chose. FallArrestBoundSystem tests the characters of the allegiance list (see SCRIPT-S328). needs: PART-3. evidence: [particles-objects.md §2026-11-02T22:39:38](../../re/particles-objects.md#ts-20261102-223938).

- SCRIPT-S328 The allegiance lists and the FallArrest update: a character enters its list at the aggro that asks for the pursuit and at the first update the AI moved it (the original enters it where a path record is created); it leaves when its object is disabled, with the boss test fed an AI request that is never 1; the airborne flag ctrl+0x314 bit 6 is always "on the ground" because the character mover and the fall are not ported; the sound's owner id is 0 (entity +0x14 is not read); the update runs after the particle objects, an order the port chose; the table is shared with the conduct of the idle goons (SCRIPT-S326), and "take the entity's own entry" and "return at once on ctrl+0xd8 bit 5" are two views of the same original routine, the path record rebuild of which is not modelled.; the engage calls of FallArrest and characterAggro do not clear the aggro counter ctrl+0x72 that the original's engage clears (all but the bit-5 return). needs: SCRIPT-430, COMBAT-8. evidence: [scripts-missions-1-4-roof.md §2026-11-03T08:42:13](../../re/scripts-missions-1-4-roof.md#ts-20261103-084213).
