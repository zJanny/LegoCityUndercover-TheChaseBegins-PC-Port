# Combat: a character takes a hit

## 1. Scope

The character side of a hit: message 0 to a level character (the hit receiver 0x1b8570, damage, the reaction message 46), the hit states PUNCHED, KNOCKED DOWN and GET UP, the takedown of the fight move's target (MELEE PUTDOWN and the takedown states), the arrest health, the list of arrestable characters and its events, the arrest by A (the player's ARRESTING, the target's ARREST and ARRESTED, CharacterArrested), and the aggro of a hit character. The player's punch and the fight move on a goon are in [player-movement-attack.md](player-movement-attack.md) (open items PLAYER-26..29); the death of a character (message 1) is [script-system-natives-world-death.md](script-system-natives-world-death.md); the goons' attack on the player (AIPursuit) is not in this file.

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-11-12T11:34:22+01:00. The hit receiver and message 46 are ported for every level character: a punch takes no health and requests PUNCHED; other hits take health and destroy the character at 0. The fight move a Y tap starts on a goon takes the goon through MELEE PUTDOWN, PUTDOWN STUN (the arrest health drops) and GENERIC GETUP back to IDLE; at arrest health 0 it is listed as arrestable and lies for its arrestable time (4 s), and CharacterArrestable is raised for the level scripts. An A tap next to an arrestable character arrests it: Chase plays `cuffing` (ARRESTING), the character ARREST and ARRESTED, CharacterArrested is raised, and one second later it is destroyed and disabled, which its AI spawner counts as done, so the first fight of mission 1-4 ends and the next wave comes. A character that has left a vehicle (the CharacterLeftVehicle event of the vehicle-exit port, or the development script step `left-vehicle`) can be arrested by an A tap at once, a stand-in for a rule not found (COMBAT-S9); its CharacterArrested starts the scripts of the mission 1-4 chase end. The counter throws (X), the grab by A of a character that is not arrestable and the other target states are not ported (COMBAT-10). The goon does not move during the takedown or the arrest. The aggro of a character the player hit acts on its AI: without a side slot the sighting (AI state 2), with one the target switch (AI state 3) and the counter. A goon that sees Chase runs up to him ([script-system-ai-navigation](script-system-ai-navigation.md)) but does not strike (COMBAT-15).
