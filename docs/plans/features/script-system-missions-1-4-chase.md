# Script system: missions, mission 1-4, the chase

## 1. Scope

The chase of mission 1-4 (`1-4_Hatch_Open` to `1-4_Car_Crash` and `1-4_RobberCaught`): the stolen car's AI driver, its hearts, the chase end, the target display and the chaser's arrest. Split from [script-system-missions-1-4](script-system-missions-1-4.md) when that file reached its size limit. The chase end flow is in [pedestrians-traffic-cars-driver](pedestrians-traffic-cars-driver.md).

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-11-12T11:30:29+01:00. In the chase, `1-4_Car_Chase` puts the stolen car's AI driver into state 5 and the car drives along `1-4_Vehicle_Path` at up to 29 m/s on its physics body (SCRIPT-422 done; speed and line not checked against the oracle); the hit of the player's vehicle on its last heart scales its body velocity and, on the fifth hit (5 hearts, one per contact above 3 m/s, at least 128 updates apart), requests driver state 6, whose entry fires VehicleChaseEnded at the car and at the level root; in `chase-game` the player's police car ends the chase this way, `1-4_Car_Crash` runs and the chaser leaves the car (CTest `game-chase-end-1-4`). VehicleSetChased fills the target display (the boss hearts object of the HUD) with the car's portrait and hearts, builds the 10-step length table of the path and runs the node test; the vehicle return of a car in driver state 5 fills the display too. Nothing draws the display (SCRIPT-462). At the end of `1-4_Hatch_Open` the booster chaser comes out of his spawner and jumps into the stolen car, hidden on its seat; `1-4_Car_Crash` takes him out again. The chase needs SCRIPT-415, SCRIPT-443, SCRIPT-445, SCRIPT-457, SCRIPT-447 and SCRIPT-462.
