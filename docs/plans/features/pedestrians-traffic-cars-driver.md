# Traffic AI driver input

## 1. Scope

The AI driver input 0x26d934 of the spawned traffic cars, the driver record and its state change, and the vehicle side of a spawn. The manager, routes and spawn are in [pedestrians-traffic-cars.md](pedestrians-traffic-cars.md), the car's physics body in [pedestrians-traffic-cars-driver-body.md](pedestrians-traffic-cars-driver-body.md).

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-11-12T08:24:22+01:00. The AI driver runs on every live traffic car in `chase-game` (`traffic_driver_connect`, TRAFFIC-2 done): the input provider, the state change and the post step with the route mover, stop test, junction choice and queue, leave call, ground probe, path follower, whistle stop and brake lights. The cars drive along their roads, stop at red lights, take the junction lanes and leave at a road end. A removal takes a car out of its junction's queue and releases a held car (message 0x80000006). The calls of states 4 to 8 are connected (`traffic_driver_states`, TRAFFIC-36 and TRAFFIC-9 done), with the body calls going to `TrafficBodyHooks`; `requestTrafficDriverState` requests a state, nothing requests one for a traffic car yet. The same driver drives the chased car of VehicleSetChased on its physics body (SCRIPT-422). The thrown objects (TRAFFIC-42) are not ported; the contact gather is in [pedestrians-traffic-cars-gather.md](pedestrians-traffic-cars-gather.md). The brake lights work: the cars show the brake light mesh while braking or waiting and start the glow effect at their rear lights, and the keep counter reads the culling bit of the car's node that the draw sets from the camera's view volume (TRAFFIC-33, TRAFFIC-37, stand-ins TRAFFIC-S35 and TRAFFIC-S36). The push of characters is ported with its world in [pedestrians-traffic-cars-push.md](pedestrians-traffic-cars-push.md), not supplied in play. Each car has its kinematic vehicle body (TRAFFIC-11 done); it and what is left of the vehicle side are in [pedestrians-traffic-cars-driver-body.md](pedestrians-traffic-cars-driver-body.md).

## 3. Verified behaviour

- Read from the code (test `ai-driver-input`), all from [traffic-ai-driver.md §2026-10-25T12:26:09](../../re/traffic-ai-driver.md#ts-20261025-122609):
  - the driver record of 0x1796e8: zero-filled, +0x74 = `TrafficHeightOffRoad`, +0x18 = the node matrix, +0xaf bit 0, kinematic body;
  - the state change 0x1cb148: +0x78 takes the request +0x79 with its leave and enter steps, and after a spawn the state goes 0 → 1 → 2, one update per step (the servant measured the same on a street with traffic after mission 1-1);

  - the post step 0x1bc79c: the junction step of state 3, the recovery 4 → 8, the break 6 → 7, the keep counter +0xad;

## 4. Stand-ins

- TRAFFIC-S35 The culling bit 18 of a car's node: the sphere is the model's bounds (not the node's own +0x9c / +0xa0), the per-node tests of the mesh visibility function, the palette flags and the box test are not ported, the camera record's scales are derived from the projection (the 3DS top screen's 400x240 aspect and the far plane before the view-distance setting), only the port's own draw sets it (the other render passes may leave a different bit), the parent test of the hidden check is not applied, and the bit is clear before the first draw; without the lights object (the drivers' unit tests) the node counts as hidden. needs: TRAFFIC-48. evidence: [traffic-car-culling.md §2026-11-12T00:12:13](../../re/traffic-car-culling.md#ts-20261112-001213)
- TRAFFIC-S36 The glow effect and the undo of the lights: the emitter has no parent node (it follows the car by its position each update, orientation identity), the undo of the lights runs when the car's entity is disabled, the brake input and the gear are those of the post step with a forward gear, and the part alpha is drawn as the instance fade scaled by the alpha (PED-S12). needs: TRAFFIC-49. evidence: [traffic-car-lights.md §2026-11-12T00:10:29](../../re/traffic-car-lights.md#ts-20261112-001029)
- TRAFFIC-S27 Facts of the connected driver the port chooses itself: the obstacle list of states 2 and 3 is every enabled boardable vehicle plus the player with the player box; the cars run in the object phase after the junction update, car by car in table order; a car keeps the room of its spawn point. needs: TRAFFIC-35. evidence: [traffic-driver-connect.md §2026-11-08T12:18:49](../../re/traffic-driver-connect.md#ts-20261108-121849)
- TRAFFIC-S33 The game clock of the whistle stop is `TrafficDriveStep::clock` and is taken as one unit per update from zero at the level start: the unit is unread (SCRIPT-384; a trap measured +0.99 and the use markers 2 per update, SCRIPT-S247 / SCRIPT-S297) and so is its value at the level start. needs: TRAFFIC-59. evidence: [traffic-car-push.md §2026-11-12T00:04:45](../../re/traffic-car-push.md#ts-20261112-000445)
- TRAFFIC-S34 The world of the push (`TrafficPushWorld`) is not supplied by the port: no car pushes a character in play. needs: TRAFFIC-56. evidence: [traffic-car-push.md §2026-11-12T00:04:45](../../re/traffic-car-push.md#ts-20261112-000445)
- TRAFFIC-S32 Facts of the connected states 4 to 8 the port chooses itself: for the chased car the whistle stop is false, the node counts as hidden, the random is its own seed, a level path route has signal 0 and no path follower, the body counts as driven (no brake of 0.7 above 5 m/s), normalGravity and wakeBody do nothing to its body; a throw has no object to throw. needs: TRAFFIC-42. evidence: [traffic-driver-states.md §2026-11-12T00:08:58](../../re/traffic-driver-states.md#ts-20261112-000858)
