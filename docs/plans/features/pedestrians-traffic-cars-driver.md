# Traffic AI driver input

## 1. Scope

The AI driver input 0x26d934 of the spawned traffic cars, the driver record and its state change, and the vehicle side of a spawn. The manager, routes and spawn are in [pedestrians-traffic-cars.md](pedestrians-traffic-cars.md).

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-11-08T12:21:05+01:00. The AI driver runs on every live traffic car in `chase-game` (`traffic_driver_connect`, TRAFFIC-2 done): the input provider, the state change and the post step with the route mover, stop test, junction choice and queue, leave call, ground probe, path follower, whistle stop and brake lights. The cars drive along their roads, stop at red lights, take the junction lanes and leave at the end of a road into no junction. A removal takes a car out of its junction's queue and releases a held car (message 0x80000006). States 4 to 8 are not reached and their calls are not connected (TRAFFIC-36): the steering `traffic_driver_steering` and the calls of states 5 and 8 in `traffic_driver_calls` (body placement, contact slide, chase markers). The brake lights have no parts yet (TRAFFIC-33) and the cop whistle is not ported (TRAFFIC-34). The vehicle side is a stand-in: no physics body, the node is set directly (TRAFFIC-S28, TRAFFIC-11).

## 3. Verified behaviour

- Read from the code, not ported: the vehicle update uses 0x26d934 for a car whose driver record has +0xaf bit 0, 0x29cd9c for the player's car and nothing for the others (0x1d8df0 is empty).
- Read from the code (test `ai-driver-input`), all from [traffic-ai-driver.md §2026-10-25T12:26:09](../../re/traffic-ai-driver.md#ts-20261025-122609):
  - the driver record of 0x1796e8: zero-filled, +0x74 = `TrafficHeightOffRoad`, +0x18 = the node matrix, +0xaf bit 0, kinematic body;
  - the state change 0x1cb148: +0x78 takes the request +0x79 with its leave and enter steps, and after a spawn the state goes 0 → 1 → 2, one update per step (the servant measured the same on a street with traffic after mission 1-1);
  - the input provider 0x26d934: the stop rule of states 2 and 3, state 5 steering 8 m ahead along the route, state 8 steering back to the path and putting the car back on it when it faces away; states 4, 6 and 7 give no input;
  - the post step 0x1bc79c: the junction step of state 3, the recovery 4 → 8, the break 6 → 7, the keep counter +0xad;
  - traffic cars are kinematic bodies (flags 0x102) except in states 4 and 6 and after a contact.
- Read from the code (tests `traffic-route-functions`, `traffic-route-functions-real-data`), from [traffic-route-functions.md §2026-11-07T18:14:58](../../re/traffic-route-functions.md#ts-20261107-181458): the route functions `ahead` 0x29fb6c (squared step lengths below an asked length of 100) and `nearest` 0x21d5b0 (samples from k = 1) of type 0, the stubs of type 1, and the arguments state 5 passes them.
- Read from the code (test `traffic-driver-steering`), from [traffic-ai-driver-steering.md §2026-11-07T12:12:03](../../re/traffic-ai-driver-steering.md#ts-20261107-121203): the controller 0x27872c with its target speed, steering and speed controllers, the placement 10 up and after 90 stuck updates, the launch byte +0x7a; the contact step 0x29cc38; the top gear speed 0x20362c.
- Read from the code (test `traffic-driver-calls`), from [traffic-ai-driver-calls.md §2026-11-08T12:12:24](../../re/traffic-ai-driver-calls.md#ts-20261108-121224): VehicleSetRaceTrim 0x286080 (+0xaf bit 2, the launch byte +0x7a, the held car 0x3745d4) and the release 0x22a1f4; the body placement 0x23091c; the contact slide 0x21a73c with mode 255 and 0x2c8f80; the chase markers of state 5 (enemy arrow and enemy group); the whistle stop 0x2c87a8 / 0x26e254; the brake lights 0x278b78 and the glow end 0x2cb42c.
- Connected (tests `traffic-driver-connect`, `traffic-system`, `traffic-system-real-data`, `game-traffic`, `game-traffic-natives`), from [traffic-driver-connect.md §2026-11-08T12:18:49](../../re/traffic-driver-connect.md#ts-20261108-121849):
  - the spawn's driver fields (0/1, route, junction, parameter, placement) and per update 0x26d934, 0x1cb148 and 0x1bc79c on every live car, the manager reading the record's bytes, speed, route and position;
  - a car on a made-up road drives to the junction, stops at its red light, goes on at green, takes the lane (queued), leaves the queue onto the output road and is removed at the end of a road into no junction;
  - the path follower 0x2bf2ec over the pregen cell under the probe; the whistle stop 0x2c87a8 stops every car while it runs;
  - the removal message 0x80000006 runs 0x22a080 for a car with a junction (0x19293c, called first by the vehicle handler 0x1797c4), then the release 0x22a1f4.
