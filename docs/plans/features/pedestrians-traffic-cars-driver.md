# Traffic AI driver input

## 1. Scope

The AI driver input 0x26d934 of the spawned traffic cars, the driver record and its state change, and the vehicle side of a spawn. The manager, routes and spawn are in [pedestrians-traffic-cars.md](pedestrians-traffic-cars.md).

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-11-12T00:13:14+01:00. The AI driver runs on every live traffic car in `chase-game` (`traffic_driver_connect`, TRAFFIC-2 done): the input provider, the state change and the post step with the route mover, stop test, junction choice and queue, leave call, ground probe, path follower, whistle stop and brake lights. The cars drive along their roads, stop at red lights, take the junction lanes and leave at the end of a road into no junction. A removal takes a car out of its junction's queue and releases a held car (message 0x80000006). States 4 to 8 are not reached and their calls are not connected (TRAFFIC-36): the steering `traffic_driver_steering` and the calls of states 5 and 8 in `traffic_driver_calls` (body placement, contact slide, chase markers). The brake lights work: the cars show the brake light mesh while braking or waiting and start the glow effect at their rear lights, and the keep counter reads the culling bit of the car's node that the draw sets from the camera's view volume (TRAFFIC-33, TRAFFIC-37, stand-ins TRAFFIC-S35 and TRAFFIC-S36). The cop whistle is not ported (TRAFFIC-34). The vehicle side is a stand-in: no physics body, the node is set directly (TRAFFIC-S28, TRAFFIC-11).

## 4. Stand-ins

- TRAFFIC-S35 The culling bit 18 of a car's node: the sphere is the model's bounds (not the node's own +0x9c / +0xa0), the per-node tests of the mesh visibility function, the palette flags and the box test are not ported, the camera record's scales are derived from the projection (the 3DS top screen's 400x240 aspect and the far plane before the view-distance setting), only the port's own draw sets it (the other render passes may leave a different bit), the parent test of the hidden check is not applied, and the bit is clear before the first draw; without the lights object (the drivers' unit tests) the node counts as hidden. needs: TRAFFIC-48. evidence: [traffic-car-culling.md §2026-11-12T00:12:13](../../re/traffic-car-culling.md#ts-20261112-001213)
- TRAFFIC-S36 The glow effect and the undo of the lights: the emitter has no parent node (it follows the car by its position each update, orientation identity), the undo of the lights runs when the car's entity is disabled, the brake input and the gear are those of the post step with a forward gear, and the part alpha is drawn as the instance fade scaled by the alpha (PED-S12). needs: TRAFFIC-49. evidence: [traffic-car-lights.md §2026-11-12T00:10:29](../../re/traffic-car-lights.md#ts-20261112-001029)
- TRAFFIC-S27 Facts of the connected driver the port chooses itself: the obstacle list of states 2 and 3 is every enabled boardable vehicle plus the player with the player box; the cars run in the object phase after the junction update, car by car in table order; a car keeps the room of its spawn point; the game clock of the whistle stop is not supplied. needs: TRAFFIC-35, TRAFFIC-34. evidence: [traffic-driver-connect.md §2026-11-08T12:18:49](../../re/traffic-driver-connect.md#ts-20261108-121849)
- TRAFFIC-S28 The vehicle side of a driven car: the kinematic switch only sets a flag, the port makes no physics body and sets the node matrix of the vehicle directly (`placeVehicleAt`); the mover's kinematic velocity and engine value are not applied; the vehicle record fields the driver writes (brake, hand brake, hearts) are kept with the driver, not in the vehicle record. needs: TRAFFIC-11. evidence: [traffic-driver-connect.md §2026-11-08T12:18:49](../../re/traffic-driver-connect.md#ts-20261108-121849)
