# Script system: platforms and attached nodes

## 1. Scope

The platform class (ObjectType 64, `props/prop_platform`, `leprops/leprop_platform`): its record, the trigger messages, the travel and the node move, and the node hierarchy of AttachObject that carries attached objects with a moving node. The natives MovePlatform, SetPlatform* and Rideable* are batch SCRIPT-37 (SetPlatformSpeed, SetPlatformOrigin, SetPlatformTarget and MovePlatform are ported here) in [script-system-natives.md](script-system-natives.md); WaitForPlatform and the generator scene of mission 1-3 are in [script-system-missions-1-3.md](script-system-missions-1-3.md).

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-10-28T00:59:56+01:00. The create, the post-link (`ObjectTo` or `Platform_Path`), the messages 0xff, 0xfe, 43, 8 (ResetObject) and 26 (MovePlatform), the request 0x2150bc and the update 0x2807c8 are read and ported for platforms without `orient` or type 7: the node moves on the lerp from its origin to its target, or on the spline of its path (arc length with `ConstantSpeed`) and turns by the squad of the path's quaternions or along the tangent; travels end by type (types 0 to 6) and carry the nodes attached below them (moves and turns). SetPlatformSpeed, SetPlatformOrigin and SetPlatformTarget are ported, and platforms register collision like kind 67. The crane platform of mission 1-3 goes down 10.63 in 102 node moves with the girder; the lawn mowers of mission 1-3 go round their paths with the gardener in his seat. The player rides a moving platform through its ground entity: on `1-3_Ledge_Platform` from y 17.463 to 30.318. On an `orient` platform the player keeps his place and heading in the platform's frame. Not yet: the platform's own sweep (push, squash, `HardTarget`, SCRIPT-388) and riding for characters the level does not attach.

## 4. Stand-ins

- SCRIPT-S301 Parts of the platform class are not ported: `orient` and type 7 do not turn the node of a platform without a path, the shape curves 1..5 and the named curves return the value unchanged, the lift timers of types 5 and 6, state 1, the speed counters, the sounds and the messages 41 to controlled platforms are missing. needs: SCRIPT-377. evidence: [props-rocking-platforms.md §2026-10-26T22:54:42](../../re/props-rocking-platforms.md#ts-20261026-225442).
- SCRIPT-S302 Carrying: attached characters take only the carried position, not the turn of a turning platform, the platforms update in the order of the level's objects (the entity order of the original is not read), and props carried by a platform that does not turn keep the rotation the rocking or their placement gives them. needs: SCRIPT-377. evidence: [props-rocking-platforms.md §2026-10-26T22:54:42](../../re/props-rocking-platforms.md#ts-20261026-225442).
- SCRIPT-S307 The path pose: the slerp takes acos and sin from the C library, not the game's own functions, the length table of type 1 paths is not ported (no path platform uses one), and the lap log line is the port's. evidence: [props-rocking-platforms.md §2026-10-28T00:06:31](../../re/props-rocking-platforms.md#ts-20261028-000631).

- SCRIPT-S309 The lift natives and the reset message 8 leave the node's rotation: SetPlatformOrigin and SetPlatformTarget do not set the quaternions R+0x30 / R+0x40, the reset neither turns the node (`orient`, `initialorientation`) nor runs the reference step, and SetPlatformSpeed with a time (SetPlatformTime) is not ported. evidence: [props-rocking-platforms.md §2026-10-28T00:09:40](../../re/props-rocking-platforms.md#ts-20261028-000940).
- SCRIPT-S310 Riding: only the player has a ground entity, only platforms become one (the other ground kinds are not ported; `orient` platforms take the frame ride), message 15 is not sent, the rise has no ceiling test, a raised probe that finds nothing ends the link without the no-ground path, the landing stun rides without slide or probe, script clip states do not ride, the ctrl+0x315 bits 2 and 4 that a non-zero turn clears are not ported, and the `orient` ride has no `chase-game` test and no oracle trace. needs: SCRIPT-388. evidence: [props-rocking-platforms.md §2026-10-28T00:09:40](../../re/props-rocking-platforms.md#ts-20261028-000940).
