# Vehicles: physics body

## 1. Scope

The physics body of a vehicle (entity+0x84, class at vtable 0x365910) and its rigid body (a Bullet 2.7x rigid body): gravity, damping, orientation and angular motion, wheels, suspension, engine, gears and tyres. Split from [vehicles.md](vehicles.md) (records, driving input and states, camera, natives); the body's collision with the level floor and the Bullet boxes and the sleep rule are in [vehicles-physics-collision.md](vehicles-physics-collision.md). Driving collision against objects and other vehicles is VEH-7 there.

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-10-15T19:07+02:00. The driven or moving vehicle is a rigid body with the original's gravity, damping, quaternion step and inverse inertia, on four suspension rays with the original's spring and damper force (VEH-30, VEH-31 closed). Engine, gears, steering and tyres are the original's (VEH-32 closed). The body collides with the level floor through the original's floor pass 0x1c54f8 on the floor graph, and sleeps by the original's rule (VEH-40, VEH-53 closed). It collides with the level's static Bullet boxes through the original's Bullet 2.78 contacts and solver and stops at the obstacle of the oracle run (VEH-60, VEH-63 closed). Still missing: contacts with other vehicles and objects (VEH-7), the sphere sweep (VEH-61), air control and some body flags (VEH-50). The braking after a jump out is not checked against a new free run (VEH-54).
