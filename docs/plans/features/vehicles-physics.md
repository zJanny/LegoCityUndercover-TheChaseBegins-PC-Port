# Vehicles: physics body

## 1. Scope

The physics body of a vehicle (entity+0x84, class at vtable 0x365910) and its rigid body: gravity, damping, orientation and angular motion, wheels, suspension, engine, gears, tyres, the body's collision with the level and the mode switch. Split from [vehicles.md](vehicles.md) (records, driving input and states, camera, natives). Driving collision against objects and other vehicles is VEH-7 there.

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-10-15T16:20+02:00. The driven or moving vehicle is a rigid body with the original's gravity, damping, quaternion step and inverse inertia, on four suspension rays with the original's spring and damper force (VEH-30, VEH-31 closed). Engine, gears, steering and tyres are the original's (VEH-32 closed): the tyres push the body at the wheel contacts, so speed, turning, squat and dive come from the tyre forces; the body steps in the original's fixed 1/30 s world steps. Collision pass and solver (VEH-40), air control and some body flags (VEH-50) are missing; the braking after a jump out is not checked against a new free run (VEH-54).
