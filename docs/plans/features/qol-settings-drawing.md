# QoL settings menu drawing

## 1. Scope

Drawing of the PC settings menu ([qol-settings-menu](qol-settings-menu.md)) with the original Options-page art: the page scene of the round menu layout, the scale into the window, the hit areas and the mapping of keys, mouse and wheel onto the menu model's events. Plan: [qol.md](../qol.md). Not a QoL feature of its own: the menu is the toggle mechanism.

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, opened 2026-10-29T16:00:26+01:00 (split from qol-settings-menu). The round form of the Game, Display and Quality of life tabs, the scale rule, the hit areas, the pointer and wheel mapping and a real-data draw test exist (QOLSET-33 part 1); the list form, the notices and the question of the Controls tab are joined into `ListForm` (`controlsListForm`, QOLSET-40); the window hook of the front end with the input wiring exists (QOLSET-41: "PC Settings" button on the slot list, front end stopped, menu over the whole window); the stores behind the open menu exist (QOLSET-49: `config/qol.cfg`, the active store, the window, `config/controls.cfg` and the running input); the Game tab runs over the live options, the mixer and the save (QOLSET-24; the Languages page is not shown, QOLSET-S6) and the pause entry (QOLSET-8) does not exist.

## 3. Verified behaviour

None against the original (the menu is not part of it). In `chase-game` the slot list shows the "PC Settings" button, a left click on it opens the menu over the whole window and the front end stops (checked by 20261029-qolhook-B under Xvfb, and by `qol-front-menu-real-data`); on the other front-end screens and in levels nothing of it runs or draws.

## 7. Code and tests

- `src/qol/menu_list_page.hpp`/`.cpp` (library `chase_qol`): Controls list, notice and question scenes and their hit areas; tests `qol-menu-list-page`, `qol-menu-list-page-real-data`.
- `src/qol/menu_hit.hpp`/`.cpp` (library `chase_qol`): `menuPageRect` and `pagePoint` (scale min(w/320, h/240), page centred), `HitArea` (box, round flag, click and hover events; shared with the list form), `roundHitAreas` and `frameHitAreas` (tap circles of the three visible slots, Back, Select, the `Pageslots` arrows, the subtitle bar hover `tabMove`), `MenuPointer` (hover only on entering an area), `eventForWheel`. Test `qol-menu-hit` (`tests/qol/menu_hit_test.cpp`): the spec's three window sizes, a tall window, the areas, hover on entering, the key and wheel mapping.
- `src/qol/menu_page.hpp`/`.cpp` (library `chase_qol_menu`, over `chase_frontend`): `menuPageSetup` (round timings and tap circles of the pause setup, Options-page labels, gametext `ON`/`OFF`, element boxes from the layouts), `rowPlateText`, `menuPageScene` (through the port's `RoundMenu`, opened at the selected row with its storyboards placed at their end), `menuFrameScene`, `menuShortcutsScene`, `ListForm` (scene, hits, overlay), `controlsListForm`, `pageScene`, `pageHitAreas`, `drawMenuPage` (page canvas at window resolution, text through the node scale, blitted centred on black). Test `qol-menu-page-real-data` (`tests/qol/menu_page_real_test.cpp`, label `needs-game-data`).
- `src/qol/menu_window.hpp`/`.cpp` (library `chase_qol_menu`): `MenuWindow` routes SDL key names (`menuKeyForName`), pad names (`menuKeyForPad`), mouse buttons, motion and wheel steps into `MenuModel` through the page scale and hit areas, binds every input while the Controls tab waits, and draws through `drawMenuPage`. Test `qol-menu-window` (`tests/qol/menu_window_test.cpp`, no game data).
- `src/qol/menu_stores.hpp`/`.cpp` (library `chase_qol`): `StoresPorts` (settings path, display modes, desktop size, window, controls and controls-changed callbacks), `MenuStores` (copy of the active store for the model, `open`, `commit` after each result: `changesSettings` writes `config/qol.cfg` and the active store, a Display change calls the window, a Controls change calls the controls callback); `WindowController::adopt`; test `qol-menu-stores` (no game data).
- `src/qol/menu_window_sdl.hpp`/`.cpp` (library `chase_qol_window`, over SDL and `chase_render`): `FrontMenu` with the slot list button (`frontButtonScene`, `opensMenu`), the bottom-screen mapping of the front presenter (`frontBottomScreen`), event routing and the whole-window present through `HudOverlay::drawTop`; hook forwards `frontMenuConsumes`, `frontMenuPresented`, `drawFrontMenuButton`. Test `qol-front-menu-real-data` (label `needs-game-data`).
- `src/qol/menu_redraw.hpp`/`.cpp` (library `chase_qol_menu`): `sceneFingerprint` and `MenuRedraw` (cache of rendered windows by window size and the fingerprints of the page, shortcuts and overlay scenes; `MenuWindow::page` returns the cached pixels and an id, `FrontMenu::present` uploads only when the id changed). Test `qol-menu-redraw-real-data` (`tests/qol/menu_redraw_real_test.cpp`, labels `needs-game-data` and `slow`): equality with the full redraw after each key, reuse, timing budgets at 960x600 and 1920x1080.
