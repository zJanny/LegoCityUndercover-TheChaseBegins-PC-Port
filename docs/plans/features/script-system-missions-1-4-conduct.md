# Script system: missions, mission 1-4, the goons' conduct (the idle decision, the sleep, the return to the spawn point)

## 1. Scope

What an AI character in the idle state does next: the decision of AI state 0 (its gate, the sleep branch, the side slots, the target search, the sight test, the sighting into AI state 2), the request of AI state 15 and state 15 itself (the wait before the return to the spawn point and its watch), and CharacterInvulnerable in the hit receiver. Split by subtopic from [script-system-missions-1-4-fight](script-system-missions-1-4-fight.md), which keeps the AI spawners, StartState and the end of the first fight. The fight itself (AI state 2) is COMBAT-9 in [combat-characters](combat-characters.md).

## 2. Status

IN_PROGRESS, updated 2026-11-07T18:25:57+01:00. Every placed character runs the decision of AI state 0: it ends while the clock 0x241098 reads 0; a character with `AISleepAnim` first sleeps (sleep clip, no search) and wakes when it sees the player (the wake clip, then awake), or at once when it moves; an idle goon takes one of its side's six slots and, once the player stands inside its `AIRangeBound` within its `AIViewDistance`, sees him and goes to AI state 2; in chase-game goons 03 and 06 see the player at `1-4_P1_GoTo` in the first update after cutscene `1_4_a`; in the other levels shown idle characters take side slots and characters with `AIMillAbout` request mill about. CharacterInvulnerable makes the receiver ignore hits. Next: the rest of the idle decision (SCRIPT-428), the walk's remaining inputs (SCRIPT-441), a run of the walk back in a real level (SCRIPT-442).
